## DECISION TREE (If Something Goes Wrong)

### Investor says no (or ghosts after first email)
```
→ Check if they're a hot lead or warm lead
→ If hot lead: Pick up phone TODAY (founder call, not email)
→ If warm lead: Move to follow-up email (Jul 22, Email 2)
→ If passing: Move on (plenty of other investors)
```

### Pilot customer doesn't respond to discovery call invite (Jul 24)
```
→ Pick up phone TODAY (founder call, not email)
→ Say: "I know you're busy. 15-min call instead of 30?"
→ Lock in time immediately (same call)
→ Send LOI within 1 hour of call end
```

### Series A only at €6M by Jul 27
```
→ Activate "stretch" outreach (phone calls to hesitant investors)
→ Offer 10% valuation discount (€9M post instead of €10M)
→ Extend close deadline to Aug 5 (communicate change immediately)
→ Do not panic. Still close-able at €8M minimum.
```

### Pilot customer wants to delay LOI signature past Jul 29
```
→ Say: "Aug 1 is deployment day. If you sign by Jul 28, you're in."
→ Do not flex on deadline (scarcity is leverage)
→ If they pass: Pivot to backup (Substack or Patreon, SAME DAY)
```

### Wire transfer not received by Jul 30 EOD
```
→ Call investor that same evening (don't wait for next morning)
→ Get wire confirmation (TXN ID + arrival time)
→ Update cap table as funds arrive (don't assume)
→ Announce close once 100% of Series A funds confirmed
```

## THINGS NOT TO DO (Anti-Patterns)

❌ **Don't wait for email responses.** Pick up phone for hot leads.  
❌ **Don't negotiate after LOI signature.** Lock terms before signature.  
❌ **Don't extend deadlines.** "Aug 1 is go/no-go. If you're not ready, next round."  
❌ **Don't over-communicate.** Email once, follow up once, then phone call.  
❌ **Don't miss daily standups.** Team needs to see you laser-focused every day.  
❌ **Don't wait for all signatures before announcing close.** Announce as soon as 80%+ is in.  
❌ **Don't take investor rejections personally.** Move fast to next investor.
