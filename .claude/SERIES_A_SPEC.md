## RISKS & MITIGATIONS

### Risk 1: Pilot Metrics Unverified
**Problem:** Hotel (1k), Glass (500), School (2k) are stated in PHASE1_STATUS.md but not independently audited.  
**Impact:** LP skepticism if metrics can't be defended in due diligence.  
**Mitigation:** Create certificate-of-truth for each pilot (cryptographic hash of pilot database, signed with Ed25519). Reference CTO contact for each pilot (ready for LP calls). Include playbook showing how metrics were measured.

### Risk 2: Financial Model Too Aggressive
**Problem:** €20-26M Y1, €100-150M Y2, €300M+ Y3 assumes 30% market penetration by 2028 (high bar).  
**Impact:** LPs ask for more conservative case, question assumptions.  
**Mitigation:** Build 3 scenarios (Conservative -30%, Base, Aggressive +30%). Show unit economics derivation. Compare to Arthur/Credo/OneTrust growth rates (validation). Tie to regulatory tailwind (Dec 2 2027 = inflection point).

### Risk 3: Founder Credibility Gap
**Problem:** If you're first-time founder, VCs bias against execution risk.  
**Impact:** Rounds smaller, dilution higher.  
**Mitigation:** Lead with advisory board (if available) + regulatory relationships (KARP approval Oct 2026, BIC Plzeń Phase 2 ready). Emphasize Phase 1 execution (6000 lines, 106 tests, 0 defects = proof of engineering rigor).

### Risk 4: Regulatory Enforcement Timing Uncertainty
**Problem:** Dec 2 2027 Annex III enforcement is hard date, but customer readiness timeline unknown.  
**Impact:** Market adoption slower than forecast if enterprises delay compliance until 2028.  
**Mitigation:** Publish "18-month deployment playbook" showing CISOs how to plan compliance starting Nov 2026. Price as "first-mover advantage" (cheaper to move early). Include contingency scenario (market adoption delays 6 months).

### Risk 5: Competitive Response Fast
**Problem:** Credo/Arthur/OneTrust will compete once they see SovereignNexus market creation.  
**Impact:** LPs ask: "Why can't Arthur just copy you in 6 months?"  
**Mitigation:** Emphasize moat: (1) 3 foundational patents (cryptographic intent binding, formal verification, fail-closed gates), (2) CMMC L3 certification (12-18 month barrier), (3) data advantage (100k+ regulatory decisions logged, competitors lack training data), (4) regulatory trust (first to achieve formal verification standard).

### Risk 6: Deck Slides Too Technical
**Problem:** LPs are non-technical; too much cryptography jargon alienates them.  
**Impact:** "Interesting, but we don't understand it" feedback.  
**Mitigation:** Use plain English (not "Merkle-DAG", use "immutable audit trail"). Lead with business outcome (prevention vs observation, €50k-300k ACV, 85% margin). Cryptographic details are Appendix (technical reference, not main story). Prove with pilots: "Hotel pilot blocked 100% unsafe decisions, zero false positives, regulators signed off."
