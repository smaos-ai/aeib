# SovereignNexus AXIOM — Investor Meeting Checklist
**Tactical Execution Guide for June 6-15 Meetings** | One-page quick reference

---

## PRE-MEETING (24 HOURS BEFORE)

### Research
- [ ] Review investor's recent investments (check Crunchbase, their website)
- [ ] Identify 1-2 companies in their portfolio that could use AXIOM
- [ ] Note any public statements on quantum security, AI governance, or defense tech
- [ ] Check for any conflicts (competitor investments, bad exits)

### Prepare Materials
- [ ] Print 3 copies of one-pager (plus extras for their team)
- [ ] Bring laptop + charger (pitch deck backup, video links ready)
- [ ] Have investor's email in your phone (follow-up within 24h)
- [ ] Prepare 5-slide overview (if they only have 20 min instead of 60)
- [ ] Write down meeting time + location + parking info
- [ ] Confirm Zoom link if virtual (send 15 min before call)

### Logistics
- [ ] Leave 30 min early for parking / traffic
- [ ] Dress: Business casual (no hoodie, no overly casual)
- [ ] Phone on silent (not vibrate)
- [ ] Bring water (they should offer, but bring backup)
- [ ] Bathroom break before meeting (don't interrupt)

### Mental Prep
- [ ] Read relevant section from `06-TALKING-POINTS.md` (investor type)
- [ ] Memorize 5 key facts (quantum timeline, patent filing, Ukraine deployment, market TAM, ask amount)
- [ ] Practice opening 30-second pitch (exact words, 1 min practice)
- [ ] Practice closing question (personalized for this investor)
- [ ] Prepare for 2-3 likely objections (have answers ready)

## OPENING (FIRST 2 MINUTES)

### Greeting
- [ ] Firm handshake, eye contact, smile
- [ ] "Hi [Name], great to meet you. Thanks for taking the time."
- [ ] Sit down, mirror their body language (open posture, not defensive)

### Small Talk (30 seconds)
- [ ] Reference mutual connection: "[Mutual contact] said great things about your work on [topic]."
- [ ] Or: "I've been following your recent investment in [company]—impressed by the [specific insight]."
- [ ] Or: "How's your day going? / Keeping you from anything important?"
- [ ] Listen more than you talk (let them speak first)

### Transition to Pitch (30 seconds)
- [ ] "I'm excited to tell you about AXIOM. It's the constitutional governance layer for AI agents."
- [ ] "The reason I wanted to meet is: post-quantum governance is happening right now, and we're 18 months ahead of competition."
- [ ] "I've got a 9-minute story. Does that work, or do you need to keep this short?"

---

## PITCH (9 MINUTES)

### Story Arc (follow `04-INVESTOR-STORY-ARC.md`)
- [ ] **Problem (2 min):** Ungoverned agents, fragmented hardware, quantum threat
- [ ] **Solution (2 min):** AXIOM Trinity (RCE, Night Cycle, IVB), constitutional layer
- [ ] **Market (1 min):** €100B+ by 2030, quantum-driven urgency
- [ ] **Traction (2 min):** Patents filed June 2, Prague PoC June 5, Ukraine + Israel deployments
- [ ] **Ask (1 min):** €10M Series A, deployment + go-to-market + R&D
- [ ] **Closing (1 min):** "Are you interested in going deeper on this?"

### Delivery Rules
- [ ] Speak slowly (you're excited, they need to follow)
- [ ] Make eye contact 70% of time (not 100%, creepy)
- [ ] Use hands (show processor stack, timeline, covenant split)
- [ ] Don't read slides (they can read too)
- [ ] Pause after key points (let it sink in)
- [ ] Hit exactly 9 minutes (have watch or phone timer visible)

### Visual Aids
- [ ] Show 1-2 visuals on laptop if available (quantum timeline, patent families)
- [ ] Or: Hand over one-pager, point to key sections
- [ ] Or: Draw processor stack on their whiteboard (if in-person)
- [ ] Don't show full 30-slide deck in initial pitch (save for deep dive)

## Q&A (5-10 MINUTES)

### Expected Questions (by investor type)

**Defense Ministry:**
- "How is this quantum-safe?" → Show Dilithium signing proof (video or demo)
- "Can this work on classified systems?" → Reference TS/CS compliance pathway
- "What's the deployment timeline?" → Ukraine live July 15, Israel Sept 30

**Finance:**
- "How does covenant enforcement work?" → Draw AP2 settlement flow
- "What's the regulatory alignment?" → SEC guidance + FINRA rule citations
- "How do we know the AI followed mandate?" → Show cryptographic audit trail example

**Healthcare:**
- "Is this FDA-approved?" → No, but it enables FDA approval (SaMD guidance alignment)
- "How does the deterministic critic work?" → Show IVB patent (convergence proof)
- "Can we use this for clinical AI?" → Yes (precision medicine capsule design partner ready)

**VC (Tier 1):**
- "Who else is working on post-quantum governance?" → Show competitive landscape (nobody else)
- "What's the competitive moat?" → Patent defensibility + first-mover advantage + cryptographic standard
- "What's the Series B timeline?" → Q1 2027, €25M (pro-rata rights for Series A investors)

### How to Answer
1. **Pause (2 seconds)** before answering (shows you're thinking, not rehearsed)
2. **Clarify the question** if needed ("Just to make sure I understand, you're asking about...?")
3. **Answer concisely** (30-60 seconds, not 5 minutes)
4. **Use proof** (show slide, reference patent, mention deployment)
5. **Redirect to next question** ("Does that answer it? Anything else?")

### Bad Answers (Avoid)
- [ ] "That's a great question" (sounds scripted)
- [ ] "We don't know yet" (if you actually do know)
- [ ] "Everyone's working on this" (they'll call you out)
- [ ] Long, rambling answers (they stop listening after 60 sec)
- [ ] Technical jargon without explanation (lost your audience)
- [ ] Defensive tone (they're not attacking you)

### Objection Handling (Likely Objections)
| Objection | Your Response | Proof |
|-----------|---------------|-------|
| "Quantum threat is overstated" | NIST confirms 2030. Quantus Index validates. Your biggest clients are asking for this. | NIST timeline + Quantus data |
| "This is too complex" | It's complex under the hood. But the mandate API is simple: approve or deny. | Show simple API example |
| "LangGraph is good enough" | LangGraph is ungoverned DAG. You can't certify mandate compliance. SEC/FDA won't accept it. | Show regulatory cites |
| "Why should I care about patents?" | Patents give you exclusivity for 20 years. First-mover advantage locks the market. | Show patent defensibility analysis |
| "When will you have revenue?" | Y2 (€2M ACV × 5 customers). Y3 (€10M ARR). Conservative path. | Show unit economics |

## CLOSING (2-3 MINUTES)

### Reading the Room
- [ ] **High interest:** "I'd like to get you a technical deep dive. When could we do a 60-min call next week?"
- [ ] **Medium interest:** "Would you like to attend the Prague PoC on June 5? It's live Dilithium signing + covenant proof."
- [ ] **Low interest:** "Even if you're not investing, would you be open to being an advisor? We value your perspective."

### Next Steps (Always Schedule)
- [ ] "Let me send you the one-pager + technical brief. Can we lock a 60-min deep dive for [date]?"
- [ ] "I'll follow up with an email tomorrow + Prague PoC link. You interested in attending June 5?"
- [ ] "When would be a good time for me to follow up? Thursday? Next Monday?"

### Commit (Get Their Commitment)
- [ ] Don't leave meeting without a next action
- [ ] **Best case:** "Let me get our CFO on a call with you Friday. That work?"
- [ ] **Medium case:** "Deep dive scheduled for June 12. I'll send prep materials Tuesday."
- [ ] **Worst case:** "I'll follow up Friday. If you're interested, we can schedule diligence."

### Final Words
- [ ] "Thanks so much for the time. This is exciting—hope you agree."
- [ ] (Stand up, handshake)
- [ ] "Safe travels / talk Friday."

### Documentation
- [ ] Log meeting in investor spreadsheet (date, time, attendees, interest level)
- [ ] Write 3-5 bullet points: key questions asked, objections raised, next steps agreed
- [ ] Note any red flags (competitor conflict, skepticism they seemed to hide, etc.)

### Follow-Up Email
- [ ] Send within 24 hours (use Template 2 from `05-EMAIL-TEMPLATES.md`)
- [ ] Subject: "[Investor Name] — AXIOM Deep Dive + Prague PoC (June 5)"
- [ ] Include: One-pager link + Prague PoC invitation + next meeting date
- [ ] CC: [Your co-founder or CEO] (not BCC—transparency)
- [ ] Don't wait for them to schedule you (you schedule the follow-up)

### Diligence Prep (If Interested)
- [ ] Prepare technical deep-dive slides (slides 11-25 from pitch deck)
- [ ] Get patent PDFs ready to share (refe to counsel)
- [ ] Prepare cap table (basic version, just current round)
- [ ] Prepare one-page team bios (founder + lead engineer minimum)

### Calendar Blocking
- [ ] Schedule deep dive (60 min, your calendar + their calendar)
- [ ] Send prep materials 48 hours before (technical brief, patent filing date, deployment roadmap)
- [ ] Confirm Zoom link 24 hours before (virtual meetings are glitchy)

## PITCH DECK EMERGENCY KIT

**If laptop dies:**
- [ ] Have one-pager printed (use it as visual aid)
- [ ] Draw processor stack on whiteboard (6 boxes: CPU/GPU/TPU/NPU/LPU/DPU)
- [ ] Recite story (you've memorized it)
- [ ] Use their whiteboard / notebook

**If they interrupt early:**
- [ ] Don't be offended (they're testing your composure + confidence)
- [ ] Answer concisely, then: "Shall I continue with the story, or jump to [specific section]?"
- [ ] Adapt on the fly (skip to market TAM if they want to know size)

**If meeting runs long:**
- [ ] Extend if they're engaged (they're interested, don't rush them)
- [ ] Cut to chase if they're disengaged (ask if they want to continue or reschedule)
- [ ] Never, ever overrun without asking ("Do you have time for 10 more minutes?")

**If they ask for a demo:**
- [ ] Don't demo in initial pitch (shows you're not confident in story)
- [ ] Reference Prague PoC: "Actually, we're doing a live demo June 5. Want to attend?"
- [ ] Or: "Happy to show you the cryptographic audit trail. Can I screen-share?"

## MENTAL RESETS (BETWEEN MEETINGS)

After each meeting, reset before the next one:

**1 Hour Before Next Meeting:**
- [ ] Drink water (stay hydrated)
- [ ] Read next investor's background (1 min)
- [ ] Review relevant talking points (2 min)
- [ ] Take 3 deep breaths (calm your nervous system)
- [ ] Check teeth / hair / coffee on shirt (quick mirror check)

**If Meeting Went Badly:**
- [ ] Don't catastrophize (one bad meeting doesn't doom Series A)
- [ ] Note what went wrong (objection you couldn't answer? Delivery stumble?)
- [ ] Fix it for next meeting (ask co-founder, or adjust pitch)
- [ ] Move on (can't change the past)

**If Meeting Went Great:**
- [ ] Don't get overconfident (they're interested, not committed)
- [ ] Don't mention other investors to them (confidentiality)
- [ ] Don't lower your ask (stay firm on valuation)
- [ ] Stay focused (one meeting doesn't close Series A)

## RED FLAGS (PAUSE & ESCALATE)

If any of these happen, contact your co-founder / advisor immediately:

- [ ] Investor asks you to misrepresent the technology (walk away)
- [ ] Investor claims to know your patentable ideas are obvious (they're testing you)
- [ ] More than 3 investors ask the same objection you can't answer (pivot pitch)
- [ ] Design partner (JPMorgan, Novartis, Intel) goes silent (follow up daily)
- [ ] Government procurement contact ghosts you (escalate to counsel / board)
- [ ] Competitor launches similar product (stay calm, emphasize moat)
- [ ] Patent filing gets delayed past June 2 (announce immediately + give timeline)
