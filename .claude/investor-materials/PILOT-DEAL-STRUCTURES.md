## PILOT-TO-ENTERPRISE PLAYBOOK

**Week 1-2: Onboarding**
- Deploy Layer 0 in pilot environment (isolated worktree)
- Train 5-12 users on pre-execution governance workflow
- Begin logging baseline compliance events

**Week 3-6: Integration**
- Integrate Layer 0 with customer's primary systems (EHR, trading platform, high-trust system)

**Week 11-12: Go-Live + Readiness**

## NEGOTIATION GUARDRAILS

**Always negotiate:**
- Payment terms (3-tranche structure: 30/35/30 or 40/40/20)
- Success criteria (pre-defined, audit-ready logs + regulatory signoff)
- Extension terms (right of first refusal for enterprise expansion)
- Reference rights (customer agrees to be reference for additional pilots)

**Never negotiate away:**
- Cryptographic governance (pre-execution mandate requirement is non-negotiable)
- Merkle-logging (all execution events must be Merkle-rooted)
- Audit trail ownership (you maintain compliance log, customer gets certified export)

**Expansion path (always plant seeds):**
- "After success on Pilot 1, we typically expand to 2-3 additional systems/desks/trials"
- "Our enterprise customers see 3-5x ROI from regulatory compliance savings alone"
- "If you go live by Dec 2026, you'll be reference customer for CMMC/MiFID II market launch in 2027"
