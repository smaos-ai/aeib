## SPECIAL TERMS

### Acquire-Out Clause (Optional)

If exit < €150M valuation before 2027:
- Investor has option to force founder buyback at original investment amount (€30M repaid over time)
- Protects investor if company underperforms

**Recommendation:** Skip this. Your €6M ARR proof by Dec 2026 is strong. This clause signals lack of confidence.

### Anti-Dilution (Standard)

Broad-based weighted average protects investor if Series B prices below Series A:

```
New price = Old price × (Pre-money valuation + New investment) / (Pre-money valuation + New investment + (Old shares × old price))
```

**Example:** If Series A @ €150M post, Series B @ €200M post, this formula prevents your investor from getting overly diluted.

### Right of First Refusal

Investor has right to lead Series B before external VCs. If investor declines, external VCs can invest.

## TERMINATION & CHANGE OF CONTROL

| Event | Treatment |
|-------|-----------|
| **M&A / Acquisition** | All preferred shares convert to common. Investor participates in exit proceeds pro-rata. Drag-along/tag-along apply. |
| **Bankruptcy** | Preferred shareholders have priority over common. Investor recovery follows liquidation preference. |
| **IPO** | Preferred converts to common automatically. Investor becomes public shareholder with pro-rata ownership. |
| **Employee Termination** | If founder/key employees leave, accelerated vesting cliff applies (12-month cliff, 4-year schedule). Board can accelerate vesting to retain talent. |
