# 83(b) Election Checklist

## HARD DEADLINE: JUL 9, 2026 — NO EXTENSIONS, NO EXCEPTIONS

**Stock issuance date:** Jun 9, 2026 (after incorporation confirmed)  
**83(b) filing deadline:** Jul 9, 2026 (30 calendar days from issuance — IRS grants zero extensions)

---

## What 83(b) Does

Elects to include unvested restricted stock in gross income at the **grant date** (not the vesting date).

| Scenario | Tax timing | Tax basis | Risk |
|----------|-----------|-----------|------|
| Without 83(b) | At each vesting event | FMV at vesting (post-appreciation) | Tax bill grows with company valuation — could be 50%+ higher at Series A |
| With 83(b) | At grant date | FMV at grant = par value ($0.0001/share) | Tax owed now = ~$0 (par × shares = negligible) |

**Example:** 4,000,000 shares at $0.0001 par = $400 taxable income at grant. Without 83(b), same shares at $1.00/share at Series A = $4,000,000 taxable income.

---

## Filing Method

**Use IRS Form 15620 — online portal (NOT fax, NOT legacy Form 83b)**  
URL: https://www.irs.gov/forms/form-15620

Steps:
1. Complete Form 15620 at the IRS portal
2. Submit online OR mail to IRS address listed on the form
3. If mailing: use **certified mail with return receipt** (USPS)
4. Retain: certified mail receipt + IRS confirmation as permanent proof

## Consequences of Missing This Deadline

- IRS does not grant extensions under any circumstances
- Founders pay ordinary income tax on stock at **each vesting event**, not at grant
- At Series A ($10M–$20M valuation): tax liability per founder could exceed $100,000–$500,000+
- This risk cannot be unwound retroactively — the only remedy is restructuring equity at significant legal cost

**DO NOT MISS THIS DEADLINE.**
