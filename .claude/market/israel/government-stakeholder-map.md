## Risk Mitigation

### Risk 1: Government Decision-Making Delays
- **Trigger:** No MoD response by Jul 15
- **Mitigation:** BIRD Foundation can escalate via direct government liaison channel
- **Fallback:** Shift focus to Innovation Authority (faster grant approval cycle)

### Risk 2: Budget Authority Disputes
- **Trigger:** MoD says pilot must wait for FY2027 budget cycle (Aug 2027)
- **Mitigation:** Request emergency/supplemental budget (national security exemption available)
- **Fallback:** Innovation Authority grant tier can fund pilot independently (€180K-€300K)

### Risk 3: Export Control Blockers
- **Trigger:** Israeli government legal counsel raises export compliance concerns
- **Mitigation:** Pre-file DCMA certification (Jul 1); prove compliance to MoD via documentation
- **Fallback:** Modify tech stack to reduce CUI classification (remove Defense Capsule if necessary, deploy base AXIOM only)

### Risk 4: Mossad Intervention / Security Clearance Requirements
- **Trigger:** Israeli government asks for security clearances, classified evaluations
- **Mitigation:** Engage Israeli defense contracts legal counsel early (Jun 15); coordinate with BIRD on standard security vetting procedures
- **Fallback:** Proceed without Mossad involvement; focus on MoD + Innovation Authority (sufficient for pilot)

### Risk 5: Competing Defense Tech Proposals
- **Trigger:** BIRD or MoD receives competing proposals from Palantir, Google, Amazon
- **Mitigation:** Emphasize sovereignty angle (open-source, Israeli data residency, transparent supply chain); BIRD expects innovation, not just scaling
- **Fallback:** Position AXIOM as complementary (governance layer, not data platform); partner with existing Israeli defense tech provider

## Appendix: Israeli Government Procurement Process

### Budget Cycle Timeline
- **FY2026:** Closed (ends Dec 31, 2026); supplemental budgets available until Sep 2026
- **FY2027:** Open for new initiatives; submission deadline Aug 31, 2026
- **FY2028:** Planning phase; long-term strategic planning in progress

### Procurement Authority Levels
- **<€100K:** Department director approval (MoD Cyber Director can approve)
- **€100K-€500K:** Divisional approval (Israeli Ministry of Defense executive board)
- **>€500K:** National security approval (Israeli National Security Council, Cabinet)

For AXIOM €180K pilot: MoD Cyber Director can approve directly (no higher-level gate). This is critical advantage.

### Standard Contract Terms (Israeli Government)
- **Liability cap:** 10-20% of contract value (standard for defense)
- **Indemnification:** Vendor responsible for IP infringement, export violations
- **Insurance:** Cyber liability (€1M-€5M recommended for Israeli government contract)
- **Data ownership:** Israeli government owns all operational data; vendor retains platform IP
- **Termination:** Either party can terminate with 30-90 days notice; Israeli government has unilateral termination for national security

### Foreign Vendor Considerations
- **Currency:** Contracts usually in USD or EUR; Israeli government may request shekel pricing
- **Language:** Hebrew translation required for official contract (English version is reference only)
- **Governance:** Disputes resolved under Israeli law (not US law); jurisdiction is Israeli courts
