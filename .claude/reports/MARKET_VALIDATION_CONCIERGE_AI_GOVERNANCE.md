## CRITICAL GAPS FOUND

### Gap 1: Pricing Too Low (40-60% below market)
- **Finding:** Enterprise AI governance platform annual contracts: $100K-500K
- **Pilot Comparable:** 90-day pilot ≈ 25% of annual value = $25-125K
- **Your Position:** €75k = ~$82K, at **lower end of range**
- **Risk:** Signals low value or incomplete offering; leaves revenue on table
- **Market Indicator:** Mid-market pilots: $5-20K/month × 3 months = $15-60K (your €75k exceeds this, but enterprise buyers expect premium for governance-first)

### Gap 2: Cryptographic Audit Trail NOT Primary Purchasing Driver (Yet)
- **Finding:** Immutable audit trails are table-stakes, not differentiators
- **Market Reality:** Agentman, FireTail, MetaComp all offer audit trails; regulators expect them, not prize them
- **Risk:** Positioning cryptographic governance as hero feature when it's becoming standard
- **Opportunity:** Frame it as "regulatory-grade provenance" (technical excellence), not primary value prop

### Gap 3: Missing AI Agent Governance Framework
- **Finding:** MetaComp launched StableX (Know Your Agent) June 2026, addressing governance-first agentic AI in finance
- **Market Gap:** No incumbent offers integrated governance layer for agentic workflows (agents + policies + audit + approval gates)
- **Emerging Segment:** Agentic AI governance expected to capture 25%+ of governance market by Q4 2026
- **Your Window:** 90 days to build vs. 6 months for competitors

### Gap 4: Pilot Duration (90 Days) May Be Too Short
- **Finding:** B2B SaaS pilots: 6-8 weeks optimal; enterprise AI governance: 90-120 days typical
- **Market Data:** Most governance platforms require 4-6 week implementation + 4-6 week evaluation = 8-12 weeks minimum
- **Risk:** 90 days may compress evaluation, reducing proof-of-value
- **Opportunity:** Position as "intensive sprint" (positional strength) or extend to 120 days

### Gap 5: Competitive Silence on Governance Positioning
- **Finding:** Hugging Face, Replicate, Together AI do NOT claim governance offerings
- **Opportunity:** Undefended market position (but also may indicate nascent demand)
- **Risk:** If market doesn't value governance deeply, first-mover advantage is minimal

### Gap 6: No Case Studies from Pilot Success
- **Finding:** Healthcare & finance have governance programs, but few published success cases of AI governance pilots
- **Market Signal:** Governance is new enough that case studies are sparse (regulatory confidentiality likely)
- **Risk:** Difficult to prove ROI until you have customer wins documented
