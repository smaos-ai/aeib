## Risk Mitigation

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-------------|--------|-----------|
| **Investor decision delays** | Medium | High | Establish 2-week decision SLA in LOI; have fallback investor list |
| **Valuation negotiation friction** | Low | Medium | Prepare valuation range pre-roadshow; board alignment on walk-away terms |
| **Technical/financial due diligence red flags** | Low | Critical | Pre-validate data room with external counsel; practice technical Q&A |
| **Competing term sheets (drag-out)** | Low | Medium | Establish "decision deadline" (June 28) in LOI negotiations |
| **Production go-live delays** | Medium | High | Parallel track with eng team; establish June 30 go/no-go criteria by June 25 |
| **Regulatory approval (EU AI Act)** | Very Low | Medium | Consult legal counsel on compliance; flag as "managed risk" in diligence materials |
