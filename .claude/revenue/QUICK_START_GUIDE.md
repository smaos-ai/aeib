1. **Finalize actual revenue** (Finance team)

   - Identify any billing delays or adjustments

2. **Update REVENUE_TRACKING_SHEET.csv**
   - Enter actual revenue for all customers
   - Add new customers closed during month
   - Update ARPU for expansions
   - Calculate cumulative MRR + ARR

**Day 10: Executive Review (60 minutes)**
1. **Revenue overview** (10 min)
   - Actual MRR vs. forecast
   - Variance analysis (if >5%, document RCA)
   - Customer count by segment
   - Year-to-date cumulative revenue

2. **Segment performance** (20 min)

   - **Platform:** Creator adoption, partnership health, churn rate
   - **Defense:** Pilot status, contract progression, deployment readiness

3. **Financial health** (10 min)
   - Gross margin (target 85%+)
   - Operating burn vs. budget
   - EBITDA / (burn) status
   - Runway confirmation (should be infinite from Oct onward)

4. **Risk & escalation** (10 min)
   - Any yellow/red flags from metrics dashboard?
   - Sales cycle extending? → Mitigation plan
   - Platform adoption slow? → Mitigation plan
   - Churn spike? → Immediate investigation + CSM response
   - COGS inflation? → Cost reduction review

5. **Next month forecast** (10 min)
   - Updated pipeline → revenue projection
   - Any changes to key assumptions?
   - Confidence level (1–5 scale)
   - Revised forecast if variance >5%

## 🎲 If Variance > 5% (What to Do)

### Step 1: Root Cause Analysis (15 min)
**Question:** Why did actual ≠ forecast?

**Check:**
- Sales cycle extended? (PoCs or pilots slipping)
- Pipeline lower than expected? (fewer PoCs started)
- Customer churn? (unexpected loss of revenue)
- COGS increased? (gross margin down)
- Platform adoption lower? (fewer creators on-boarded)
- Defense pilot delayed? (contract signature pushed)

### Step 2: Sensitivity Analysis (10 min)
**Reference:** REVENUE_FORECAST_DETAILED.md "Sensitivity Analysis" section

**Find matching scenario:**
- Scenario 1 (Sales cycle extends) → Actions: Accelerate SDR, launch reseller
- Scenario 2 (Platform slow) → Actions: Add partnerships, creator incentives
- Scenario 3 (Government slips) → Actions: No action; profit still healthy Jan+

### Step 3: Mitigation & Forecast Adjustment (15 min)
**Update forecast based on RCA:**
- If sales cycle extended 2 weeks → shift Customer #5 from Dec to Jan → lower Dec MRR by €40k
- If platform adoption 40% slower → lower Platform revenue by €40–60k → total MRR miss €40–100k
- If defense pilot delayed → government revenue pushed to Jan → tolerable (profit still strong)

**Prepare mitigation actions:**
- Sales team: Can you accelerate PoCs by 2 weeks? (if sales cycle slipping)
- Platform team: Can you add incentive launch or new partners? (if adoption slow)
- Defense: Is Q1 2027 pilots still on track? (if pilot delayed)

### Step 4: Board Communication (if needed)
**If variance >15%, prepare board update:**
- What happened? (RCA summary)
- Impact on Dec target? (revised forecast)
- Mitigations in place? (actions + timeline)
- New forecast confidence? (1–5 scale)
- Series A impact? (runway, profitability timeline)

### Reference Documents:
- **REVENUE_FORECAST_DETAILED.md** — "Downside Scenarios" section
- **REVENUE_METRICS_DASHBOARD.md** — "Contingency Triggers & Pivot Plans" section

## 🚨 If Red Flag Triggered (Immediate Action)

### Customer Churn >5% Monthly
**Action: Within 24 hours**
1. VP CS calls all churned customers (or at-risk customers)
2. Identify reason (product fit, pricing, competitor, support)
3. Propose mitigation (discount, feature add, implementation support)
4. Document in CRM + bring to CSM weekly

**Escalate if:** Churn persists >2 months or affected revenue >€20k/month

### Sales Cycle >12 Weeks
**Action: Within 48 hours**
1. VP Sales reviews all PoCs in progress
2. Identify which ones are slipping (PoC extended? Pilot delayed?)
3. Propose acceleration (compress scope, increase resources, executive engagement)
4. Set new close target (with realistic dates)

**Escalate if:** 3+ PoCs slip >2 weeks

### Gross Margin <75%
**Action: Within 1 week**
1. Finance reviews COGS breakdown (cloud, support, payment processing)
2. Identify which cost category spiked
3. Propose mitigation (negotiate cloud pricing, reduce support scope, automate)
4. Target: Restore to 85%+ by next month

**Escalate if:** Margin remains <80% after mitigation plan

### Platform Adoption <50% of Forecast
**Action: Within 1 week**
1. VP Platform reviews partnership health (creator satisfaction, onboarding friction)
2. Identify if onboarding is slow or creators are churning
3. Propose intervention (creator incentives, faster onboarding, new partnerships)
4. Set recovery timeline (to hit target by month-end or accept shortfall for next month)

**Escalate if:** Adoption remains <60% after intervention

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **REVENUE_TRACKING_SHEET.csv is your source of truth**
   - Update daily with closures, expansions, churn
   - Use for all reporting (board, investors, team)
   - Keep version history (e.g., revenue-actual-2026-08.csv)

2. **Variance >5% requires investigation**
   - Use REVENUE_FORECAST_DETAILED.md "Sensitivity Analysis" to find cause
   - Use REVENUE_METRICS_DASHBOARD.md "Contingency Triggers" to find action
   - Document RCA in monthly board update

3. **Green/yellow/red thresholds are your early warning system**
   - Green = on track, no action
   - Yellow = monitor, early intervention
   - Red = immediate action, board escalation
   - Reference REVENUE_METRICS_DASHBOARD.md "Decision Gates" section

4. **Monthly cadence is the heartbeat**
   - Days 1–5: Close + reconcile
   - Day 10: Executive review (60 min)
   - Day 15: Board update
   - Day 20: Forecast refresh (if >5% variance)

5. **Build safety into the model**
   - Conservative forecast (current plan) is base case
   - 30% buffer built into sales cycle (7 ± 3 weeks acceptable)
   - Platform ramp assumptions tested for 50% adoption shortfall
   - Defense delays don't break profitability (Jan 2027+ still strong)

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: What if we miss €500k by December?**
A: Framework is conservative. Downside scenario (30% miss) still hits €350k MRR = profitable. Upside scenario (4% beat) hits €520k MRR. Plan for 85% confidence in €500k target.

**Q: What if a major customer churns?**
A: Enterprise customers have 100% retention to date. Risk low but monitored. If churn occurs, immediate CSM response + board escalation. Impact: €50k–100k revenue loss (mitigated by platform/defense growth).

**Q: What if sales cycle extends to 10 weeks?**
A: 2-week extension pushes Customer #5 from Dec to Jan. Total impact: €40–50k MRR shortfall in Dec. Acceptable if pipeline acceleration (new PoCs) or platform growth offsets. Documented in sensitivity analysis.

**Q: What if Platform creators don't adopt?**
A: Worst-case scenario (40% adoption miss) = €40–60k shortfall on platform. Mitigated by: 1) Creator incentives, 2) New partnerships, 3) Vertical diversification. Still hit €400k+ MRR (87% of target).

**Q: What if government pilots slip to Q1 2027?**
A: No permanent revenue loss. Timing shift only. Jan 2027 profitability still €175k+/month from Fortress + Platform. Government adds €100k+/month Jan onward (upside).

**Q: Do we need Series A to hit €500k?**
A: Yes. Series A (€10M) funds sales team scaling (2 AEs), product hardening, infrastructure. Without Series A, sales velocity capped at current team (would hit €300k by Dec, not €500k).
