## Operational Processes

### Monthly Revenue Review (60 minutes)

**Participants:** CEO, CFO, VP Sales, VP Platform, VP CS

**Agenda:**
1. **Revenue close** (10 min): Actual MRR vs. forecast, customer adds/churn/expansion
2. **Segment reviews** (20 min): Fortress pipeline, Platform partnerships, Defense pilots
3. **Financial health** (10 min): Gross margin, burn, runway, profitability trajectory
4. **Risk & escalation** (10 min): Any early warning signals, intervention actions
5. **60-day outlook** (10 min): Pipeline progression, hiring needs, forecast update

**Outputs:**
- Updated MRR + ARR figures
- Variance analysis (any >5% variance = RCA + mitigation plan)
- Board/investor communication package
- Next month forecast refresh

### Daily Operations
- **Live tracking:** REVENUE_TRACKING_SHEET.csv updated with customer closures, expansions
- **Pipeline velocity:** Sales team logs PoC starts, pilot completions, contract signatures
- **Customer health:** CSM monitors adoption, support tickets, expansion signals
- **Churn alerts:** Any customer showing <20% engagement = immediate outreach

### Quarterly Strategic Review
- **Burndown to profitability:** Progress toward €350k MRR breakeven (Sep target)
- **Unit economics validation:** CAC, LTV, payback period, margin trends
- **Market feedback:** Customer win/loss analysis, competitive positioning
- **Budget allocation:** Sales hiring, product investment, infrastructure scaling
