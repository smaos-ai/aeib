## Monthly Review Protocol

### Review Cadence
- **Monthly close:** First 5 business days (revenue finalization, CSM check-ins)
- **Monthly review:** Day 10 (executive review + forecast update)
- **Stakeholder communication:** Day 15 (board + investor update)

### Monthly Review Agenda (60 minutes)
1. **Revenue update** (10 min)
   - Actual MRR vs. forecast
   - Customer adds, churn, expansion
   - Pipeline velocity

2. **Segment deep dives** (20 min)
   - Fortress: Win rates, sales cycles, expansion
   - Platform: Creator adoption, partnership health
   - Defense: Pilot status, conversion probability

3. **Financial health** (10 min)
   - Gross margin, COGS trends
   - Operating burn vs. budget
   - Runway, profitability trajectory

4. **Risk & mitigation** (10 min)
   - Early warning signals
   - Intervention actions (if needed)
   - Forecast adjustment

5. **Next 60-day outlook** (10 min)
   - Pipeline stage progression
   - Key milestone targets
   - Resource/hiring needs

### Reporting & Visibility
- **Live dashboard:** REVENUE_TRACKING_SHEET.csv (updated daily)
- **Monthly report:** Delivered to board + investors by day 15
- **Variance analysis:** Any >5% variance = RCA + mitigation plan
