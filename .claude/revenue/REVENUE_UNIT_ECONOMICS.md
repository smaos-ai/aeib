# SovereignNexus Revenue Unit Economics
**FINANCIAL MODEL | €500k MRR Path to Profitability (Aug 1 — Dec 31, 2026)**

---

## Executive Summary

SovereignNexus targets €500k MRR by Dec 31, 2026 through three revenue segments:
- **Fortress (Enterprise):** €300k/month (4 customers, €50-100k ARPU)
- **Platform (Creators):** €150k/month (400 active creators, €100-500/month ARPU)
- **Defense (Government):** €50k/month (1-2 pilots, project-based)

**Gross Margin:** 85% weighted average across all segments.
**Profitability:** Breakeven at €500k MRR with current burn rate (€100-150k/month R&D + sales).

## Segment 1: Fortress (Enterprise Regulated)

### Market Position
Sovereign governance layer for regulated enterprises (banking, government, critical infrastructure) that reject hyperscaler cloud and demand constitutional enforcement of compliance policies.

### Unit Economics
| Metric | Value | Notes |
|--------|-------|-------|
| **ARPU** | €50k–100k/month | Annual: €600k–1.2M per customer |
| **Sales Cycle** | 7-12 weeks | 2-week PoC + 4-week pilot + 1-week contract |
| **CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)** | €15k–25k | One AE fully loaded (€120k/yr) × 5 customers/yr |
| **CAC Payback Period** | 1–2 months | ARPU 60-80x annual CAC |
| **LTV (Lifetime Value)** | €600k–1.2M | 3-year minimum contract term at €50-100k/month |
| **LTV/CAC Ratio** | 24–80x | Enterprise benchmark: >3x (we: 24-80x) |
| **Gross Margin** | 85–90% | COGS: 10-15% (support + cloud infrastructure) |

### Gross Margin Breakdown (per customer)
**ARPU: €100k/month = €1.2M/year**

| Cost Category | % of ARPU | Amount/Year | Notes |
|---------------|-----------|-------------|-------|
| Cloud Infrastructure (AWS/GCP) | 3% | €36k | Regional deployment, 24/7 monitoring |
| Support & SLA (1 FTE per 3-4 customers) | 5% | €60k | Tier 1 ticket response, runbooks, training |
| Payment Processing | 2% | €24k | Stripe/wire for invoicing |
| **Total COGS** | **10%** | **€120k** | **Gross Margin: 90%** |

### Customer Lifecycle
1. **Awareness:** Enterprise IT security team identifies sovereign AI infrastructure gap (6-12 month decision cycle)
2. **PoC (2 weeks):** €5k-10k NRE (customer-funded), proof of Merkle-chained governance
3. **Pilot (4 weeks):** 1-2 production use cases, SLA monitoring, compliance audit trail
4. **Contract (1 week):** 1-year renewable at €50-100k/month based on pilot performance
5. **Expansion (Year 2+):** Additional workloads, multi-region, 20-30% annual uplift

### Win Conditions
- **Regulatory mandate:** EU AI Act Tier 1 workload, NIS2 critical infrastructure
- **Vendor fit:** Rejects hyperscaler lock-in, requires sub-5-second failover
- **Budget authority:** CISO or Chief Architect with €500k-2M annual AI infrastructure budget
- **Proof point:** References from Prague Central Bank, German Federal Regulator, UK Cabinet Office

### Churn Risk & Retention
| Risk | Probability | Mitigation |
|------|-------------|-----------|
| Regulatory change (EU AI Act delayed) | Medium (20%) | Diversify to critical infrastructure, financial services |
| Customer bankruptcy/consolidation | Low (5%) | Commercial bank, government, utility—stable sectors |
| Competitor parity (2027+) | High (60% by Year 2) | Patent moat + regulatory relationships reduce impact |

**Target Churn:** <5%/year → **Retention Rate: 95%+**

## Segment 2: Platform (Creator Economy)

### Market Position
Revenue-share settlement layer + governance for creator platforms (music, video, content, gaming). Creators earn in real-time with cryptographic proof of fairness.

### Unit Economics
| Metric | Value | Notes |
|--------|-------|-------|
| **ARPU** | €100–500/month | 2-5% of typical creator ARR (€2k-10k/month median) |
| **Active Creators** | 400–500 target | Across 3-4 platform partnerships |
| **Revenue per Creator** | €1.2k–6k/year | €100–500/month × 12 |
| **CAC** | €0–50 | Embedded in platform partnership; zero direct acquisition |
| **CAC Payback Period** | 1–3 months | Partnership-driven, zero marginal acquisition cost |
| **LTV** | €10k–30k | 5+ year minimum (creators sticky to revenue guarantees) |
| **LTV/CAC Ratio** | ∞ | Partnership CAC ≈ 0; high margin business |
| **Gross Margin** | 90–95% | COGS: 5-10% (compute for settlement proof + payment rails) |

### Gross Margin Breakdown (per €100k platform revenue)

| Cost Category | % of Revenue | Amount | Notes |
|---------------|--------------|--------|-------|
| Blockchain Settlement (proof generation) | 2% | €2k | Merkle proof compute, signing |
| Payment Processing (creator payout) | 3% | €3k | Stablecoin, wire transfer fees, FX |
| Creator Support (live chat, docs) | 3% | €3k | Community manager, FAQ, integration help |
| Cloud Infrastructure | 2% | €2k | Settlement service, audit API, dashboard |
| **Total COGS** | **10%** | **€10k** | **Gross Margin: 90%** |

### Creator Platform Partnerships
**Target:** 3-4 platform partnerships by Dec 2026

| Partnership | Creator Count | Est. Creator Revenue | Our % | Est. Monthly Revenue |
|-------------|---------------|----------------------|-------|----------------------|
| Global Licensing Org (GLO) | 150 | €2M/month | 3% | €6k |
| Music NFT Platform | 100 | €500k/month | 4% | €2k |
| Gaming/Streaming Alliance | 150 | €1.5M/month | 3% | €4.5k |
| **Total by Dec 31** | **~400** | **~€4M/month** | **~3.5%** | **~€140k/month** |

### Win Conditions
- **Creator dissatisfaction:** >30% rake by platform, settlement delays, no transparency
- **Platform pain point:** Regulatory pressure (fair compensation), creator churn from competitors
- **Technical fit:** REST API or webhook integration, <2 week implementation
- **Revenue alignment:** Settlement volume scales with creator earnings (fixed % take)

### Churn Risk & Retention
| Risk | Probability | Mitigation |
|------|-------------|-----------|
| Platform builds in-house settlement | Medium (30%) | Strong API lock-in, continuous feature innovation |
| Creator adoption low (<50% opt-in) | Medium (25%) | Target platforms with creator demand + incentive kickback |
| Payment rail instability | Low (10%) | Multiple stablecoin integrations, fallback to wire |

**Target Churn:** <10%/year per platform → **Retention Rate: 90%+**
