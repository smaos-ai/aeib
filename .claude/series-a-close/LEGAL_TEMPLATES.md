# Series A Close: Legal Templates & Term Sheet Framework

---

## 1. TERM SHEET FRAMEWORK (Investor-Specific Version)

### Template: Simplified Term Sheet Outline

**TO:** [COMPANY NAME]  
**FROM:** [INVESTOR NAME]  
**DATE:** [DATE]  
**RE:** Series A Preferred Stock Financing - Non-Binding Term Sheet

---

### KEY TERMS

#### 1. Investment Amount & Valuation
- **Total Investment:** €[X] (e.g., €270,000)
- **Post-Money Valuation:** €[Y] (e.g., €1,080,000)
- **Percentage Ownership:** [Z]% (e.g., 20%)
- **Per Share Price:** €[P] (derived from valuation)

#### 2. Security & Terms
- **Security:** Series A Preferred Stock
- **Liquidation Preference:** [1x non-participating / 1x participating / 2x]
  - *Recommended:* 1x non-participating (founder-friendly, standard at early stage)
- **Participation Cap:** [Specify if applicable, e.g., "uncapped" or "2x investment"]

#### 3. Governance & Board Representation
- **Board Seats (Investor):** 1
- **Board Seats (Founder):** 2 (or per existing agreements)
- **Board Seat Appointment:** Right to designate director upon investment
- **Observer Rights:** [Yes/No] for non-board investors

#### 4. Protective Provisions (Investor Veto Rights)
Investor consent required for:
- [ ] Liquidation, merger, sale of company
- [ ] Increase/decrease authorized shares (without proportional adjustment)
- [ ] Creation of new class of senior/pari passu preferred stock
- [ ] Amendment to certificate of incorporation affecting Series A
- [ ] Distributions or dividends (other than pro rata)
- [ ] Change in size of board (unless proportional)
- [ ] Related-party transactions >€[X]
- [ ] Annual budget >€[X] variance from board-approved plan

#### 5. Anti-Dilution & Participation Rights
- **Anti-Dilution Protection:** [Weighted average / Broad-based weighted average]
  - *Recommended:* Weighted average (less severe to founders)
- **Pro-Rata Rights:** Investor may invest in future rounds to maintain ownership %
  - *Typical:* Up to [100%] of investor's current ownership
- **Participation Right:** If Series B occurs, investor has right to participate
- **Follow-On Preference:** [Specify any special preferences in future rounds]

#### 6. Stock Option Pool & Employee Equity
- **Option Pool:** [X]% of post-money valuation reserved for future employee grants
  - *Typical:* 15-20% (covers 3-4 years of hiring)
- **Vesting Schedule:** [4-year vest, 1-year cliff] (standard)
- **Acceleration:** [X% upon change of control, % upon termination without cause]

#### 7. Registration & Liquidity Rights
- **Demand Registration Rights:** [Specify if applicable for later-stage investors]
- **Piggyback Rights:** Investor may piggyback on founder IPO registration
- **S-3 Rights:** If available, investor may require company to file S-3 registration
- **Lock-up Period:** [Specify if applicable, typically 180 days post-IPO]

#### 8. Information & Inspection Rights
- **Quarterly Financial Statements:** Unaudited balance sheet, P&L, cash flow within [30] days of quarter-end
- **Annual Financial Statements:** Audited financials within [90] days of year-end
- **Annual Budget:** Within [30] days of new fiscal year
- **Inspection Rights:** Right to inspect books/records [monthly/quarterly/annually]
- **Board Meetings:** Attend all board meetings, receive materials [X] days in advance

#### 9. Right of First Refusal & Co-Sale
- **ROFR:** Investor has right to purchase pro-rata share in any new equity issuance
- **Co-Sale (Tag-Along):** If founders sell shares, investor may participate proportionally
- **Drag-Along:** If investors owning [75%+] approve sale, all shareholders must participate

#### 10. Representations & Warranties (Summary)
Company represents:
- [ ] Duly organized and validly existing
- [ ] Authorized to conduct business
- [ ] No material litigation pending
- [ ] Financial statements are accurate
- [ ] No undisclosed liabilities
- [ ] IP properly assigned to company
- [ ] No material adverse changes since [DATE]

#### 11. Conditions to Closing
- [ ] Cap table audit complete and verified
- [ ] 409A valuation obtained
- [ ] All IP assignment agreements executed
- [ ] Legal opinion from counsel to company
- [ ] Employment agreements for all employees
- [ ] Board resolutions authorizing issuance
- [ ] Updated cap table reflecting new investment
- [ ] Certificate of incorporation amended for Series A
- [ ] Satisfactory legal due diligence completed
- [ ] Material adverse change search clean

#### 12. Use of Proceeds
- **Total Allocation:** €[X] (100%)
  - Product Development: €[Y] ([%])
  - Sales & Marketing: €[Z] ([%])
  - Operations & Infrastructure: €[W] ([%])
  - Working Capital: €[V] ([%])

#### 13. Expenses & Costs
- **Company Responsible For:**
  - Company legal counsel (~€[X])
  - Accounting/audit (~€[X])
  - Company D&O insurance (~€[X])
  
- **Investor Responsible For:**
  - Investor legal counsel
  - Investor due diligence costs

#### 14. Exclusivity & Confidentiality
- **Exclusivity Period:** [30] days from term sheet date
  - Company agrees not to solicit competing term sheets
  - Exception: Unsolicited superior proposals (with board consideration period)
- **Confidentiality:** Both parties keep terms confidential except to legal/tax advisors

#### 15. Closing & Effective Date
- **Closing Date:** Within [15] business days of all conditions satisfied
- **Effective Date:** [DATE]
- **Series A Shares Issuable:** Upon closing

#### 16. Binding vs. Non-Binding
- **Non-Binding Except:** Exclusivity, confidentiality, ROFR, expense reimbursement
- **Binding Upon:** Execution by authorized representatives of both parties

## 3. CLOSING CHECKLIST: LEGAL DOCUMENTATION

### Pre-Closing Verification (Due Diligence)

#### Cap Table & Capitalization
- [ ] **409A Valuation:**
  - Document: 409A valuation opinion from approved provider
  - Date obtained: ________
  - Valuation: €________
  - Prepared by: ________
  - Board minutes approving: Date ________

- [ ] **Cap Table Audit:**
  - All common shares issued and authorized: [X] shares
  - All preferred shares (Series A) registered: [X] shares
  - Option pool size: [X]% of post-money
  - Unvested shares clearly marked with vesting schedule
  - No secret equity, side letters, or off-books options
  - Total capitalization accounts for 100%

- [ ] **Outstanding Equity Summary:**
  ```
  Common Stock:
    Founder A: X shares (Y%)
    Founder B: X shares (Y%)
    Employees: X shares (Y%)
    Reserved (options): X shares (Y%)
  ---
  Total pre-money: X shares (100%)
  
  Series A Preferred:
    Investor (new): X shares (20% post-money)
  ---
  Total post-money: X shares (100%)
  ```

#### IP & Intellectual Property
- [ ] **IP Assignment Agreements:**
  - All founders have signed IP assignment to company
  - All employees have signed invention assignment (per employment agreement)
  - No carve-outs for prior IP or side projects
  - Founder agreement covers:
    - Inventions created during tenure
    - Works of authorship
    - Patents, copyrights, trademarks
    - Technology developed on company equipment

- [ ] **Patent/Trademark Filings:**
  - List any patents filed: [LIST]
  - List any trademarks filed: [LIST]
  - All documents in company name (not founder names)
  - No disputes or oppositions pending

- [ ] **Code & Software Ownership:**
  - GitHub repos confirmed company-owned
  - No GPL or viral open-source licenses in proprietary code
  - Third-party dependencies documented (compliance check)

#### Corporate Governance
- [ ] **Certificate of Incorporation / Articles of Association:**
  - Amended to authorize Series A preferred stock class
  - Includes standard protective provisions
  - Authorized capital sufficient for investment + option pool

- [ ] **Board Resolutions:**
  ```
  BOARD RESOLUTIONS TO OBTAIN:
  1. Authorization of Series A Preferred Stock issuance
  2. Approval of 409A valuation
  3. Approval of investment terms & term sheet
  4. Approval of new director (investor representative)
  5. Approval of stock option grants (if new grants post-close)
  6. Approval of use of proceeds
  7. Ratification of all IP assignment agreements
  8. Authorization for CEO to execute closing documents
  ```

- [ ] **Bylaws/Operating Agreement:**
  - Updated to reflect new board seat
  - Shareholder voting procedures documented
  - Quorum and voting thresholds confirmed
  - Anti-dilution provisions clear

#### Employment & Equity
- [ ] **Employment Agreements:**
  - All employees have signed agreements
  - Includes IP assignment language
  - Confidentiality and non-compete (if enforceable in jurisdiction)
  - At-will employment stated
  - No side letters or verbal promises

- [ ] **Option Pool & Grants:**
  - Option pool established pre-closing (if not already)
  - Size: [X]% of post-money cap
  - Board minutes approving pool
  - Stock option plan approved (401(k) plan if US-based, Section 2(a) if EU)
  - Vesting schedules: 4-year vest, 1-year cliff (standard)
  - No acceleration provisions that conflict with investor terms

- [ ] **Founder Vesting:**
  - Founder common shares on [4-year, 1-year cliff] vesting
  - OR double-trigger acceleration upon change of control
  - Documentation: side letters or option agreement

#### Contracts & Liabilities
- [ ] **Material Contracts Listed:**
  - All contracts >€[50K] listed and reviewed
  - Customer contracts: [LIST with values]
  - Supplier contracts: [LIST]
  - Partnership agreements: [LIST]
  - No change-of-control termination clauses that trigger on Series A

- [ ] **Litigation Search:**
  - Litigation search completed (clean results)
  - No pending litigation, arbitration, or disputes
  - No regulatory investigations
  - No material unresolved complaints

- [ ] **Lease & Real Estate:**
  - Office lease (if applicable): [ADDRESS, TERM, MONTHLY COST]
  - No restrictions on assignment
  - Landlord consent not required for change of control

#### Tax & Compliance
- [ ] **Tax Compliance:**
  - Federal/state tax returns filed (last 3 years)
  - No back taxes owed
  - Tax audit history: [NONE / PENDING / RESOLVED]
  - Tax ID confirmed

- [ ] **Employment Tax:**
  - W4s (US) or equivalent on file for all employees
  - Payroll tax deposits current
  - No wage/hour violations

- [ ] **Regulatory Compliance:**
  - No licenses/permits issues
  - Compliance with data protection (GDPR, CCPA) if applicable
  - No sanctions/export control violations

### Documents Required for Closing (Standard Series A)

- [ ] **Wire Transfer Authorization:**
  - Bank name, account number, routing number
  - Amount: €________
  - Timing: [Upon satisfaction of closing conditions / simultaneous with company documents]
  - Contact for wire coordination: ________

**COMPANY TO DELIVER:**

#### Stock & Capitalization
- [ ] **Stock Certificate (Series A):**
  - Investor name as registered owner
  - Number of shares: ________
  - Series A preferences documented
  - Issued date: [CLOSING DATE]
  - Signed by authorized officer

- [ ] **Updated Cap Table:**
  - All shares reflected post-closing
  - Ownership percentages updated
  - Signed by CFO/Board Secretary
  - Dated as of closing date

- [ ] **Stock Ledger:**
  - All share issuances documented
  - Series A shares added
  - Signed by Secretary/authorized officer

#### Legal Agreements
- [ ] **Series A Preferred Stock Agreement:**
  - Rights, preferences, privileges of Series A
  - Liquidation preferences
  - Participation rights
  - Anti-dilution provisions
  - Signed by all parties

- [ ] **Stockholders' Agreement (if multi-investor):**
  - Pro-rata rights, co-sale, drag-along
  - ROFR, tag-along, anti-dilution provisions
  - Signed by all shareholders and investor

- [ ] **Investor Rights Agreement:**
  - Information & inspection rights
  - Board observation rights
  - Registration rights (if applicable)
  - Signed by company and investor

- [ ] **Board Resolutions (Original Executed Copies):**
  - Authorization of Series A issuance
  - Approval of investment terms
  - Appointment of investor director
  - Stock incentive plan approval
  - Use of proceeds approval

- [ ] **Amended & Restated Certificate of Incorporation:**
  - Series A class created
  - Preferred stock provisions
  - Signed by Secretary, board approval documented
  - Filed with secretary of state (as applicable)

#### Tax & Financial
- [ ] **Legal Opinion from Company Counsel:**
  - Confirms company valid and existing
  - Confirms authority to issue securities
  - Confirms no litigation
  - Opine on state law compliance
  - Signed by outside counsel

- [ ] **Officer Certificate:**
  - CEO/CFO certifies financial statements are accurate
  - Certifies no material adverse changes
  - Certifies all representations & warranties true
  - Dated as of closing date

- [ ] **CFO Certificate (Use of Proceeds):**
  - Allocation of investment proceeds
  - Board approval of use documented
  - Cash flow impact modeled

- [ ] **Tax Representation (IRS Form 8832 if applicable):**
  - Corporation taxed as C-corporation (US)
  - EU entity structure clarified if applicable

#### Post-Closing Administrative
- [ ] **Capitalization Table (Final Post-Closing Version):**
  - All shareholders and ownership %
  - Board composition (# seats per investor)
  - Option pool remaining capacity

- [ ] **Board Minutes (Post-Closing):**
  - Acceptance of new director (investor representative)
  - Acknowledgment of new capitalization
  - Approval of investor resolutions
  - Dated and signed after closing

- [ ] **Investor Reporting Portal Setup (if applicable):**
  - Login credentials provided
  - Initial cap table uploaded
  - Reporting schedule confirmed (quarterly)

## 5. POST-CLOSING CHECKLIST (Within 30 Days)

### Administrative & Legal
- [ ] **File Amended Certificate of Incorporation** (if US-based)
  - Delaware: File with DE Secretary of State
  - Or equivalent in jurisdiction of incorporation
  
- [ ] **Update Stock Ledger & Minute Book**
  - All closing documents filed in minute book
  - Stock certificates stored safely
  - Cap table printed and signed by all parties

- [ ] **Investor Onboarding:**
  - Investor director credentials established (if applicable)
  - Board meeting schedule shared (monthly / quarterly)
  - Reporting cadence confirmed
  - Any investor advisory matters documented

- [ ] **Cap Table Distribution:**
  - Final post-closing cap table distributed to all shareholders
  - All shareholders sign cap table acknowledgment

### Financial & Accounting
- [ ] **Cash Receipt & Bank Deposit:**
  - Wire received and deposited
  - Bank account reflects new balance
  - Accounting entry recorded (debit: cash, credit: preferred stock)

- [ ] **Use of Proceeds Tracking:**
  - Create cost center / budget tracking for Series A proceeds
  - Monthly tracking against board-approved plan
  - Reporting template established

- [ ] **Update Financial Projections:**
  - Incorporate new runway from Series A
  - Update financial model with new investor assumptions

### Employee & HR
- [ ] **All-Hands Meeting:**
  - Announce funding round to entire company
  - Clarify equity impact on existing employees:
    - Option pool not affected
    - Existing shares diluted by [X]%
    - New valuation: €[X]
    - Post-money cap table shared (summary)

- [ ] **Equity Communication:**
  - Email to all option holders clarifying new cap table
  - 409A valuation update for future option grants
  - Offer to discuss equity with interested employees

- [ ] **Option Grant Approvals:**
  - If new employee hiring planned post-close
  - Board approval of new option grants (from expanded pool)
  - Grant documents signed

### Investor & Board Management
- [ ] **First Investor Board Meeting:**
  - Schedule within 30 days of closing
  - Provide board package 5 business days prior:
    - Financial statements (current month YTD)
    - Company updates (product, customer, operational)
    - Metrics dashboard
    - Upcoming milestones
    - Use of proceeds progress

- [ ] **Investor Reporting Schedule:**
  - Quarterly financials (unaudited)
  - Quarterly board meetings
  - Annual audited financials
  - Investor portal for self-service access

## 6. RISK MITIGATION: COMMON CLOSING DELAYS

| Issue | Prevention | Contingency |
|-------|-----------|-------------|
| **Missing IP assignment** | Collect all signed agreements pre-close | Escrow portion of proceeds until signed |
| **Cap table disputes** | Audit pre-close, get unanimous board approval | Escrow until dispute resolved (30 days) |
| **Employment agreement gaps** | Sign all agreements before closing | Obtain signed agreements from key employees |
| **Litigation/litigation holds** | Clean search pre-close, update on close day | Escrow portion of proceeds for 90 days |
| **Material adverse change** | Investor reps due diligence | Proceed unless material impact to business model |
| **Investor legal review delays** | Exchange full doc package 3 weeks pre-close | Set firm deadline: "docs final by [DATE]" |
