## 5. VALUATION DEFENSE FRAMEWORK

**Current Traction:** €180K ARR
**Target Valuation Range:** €1.08B - €1.44B (6-8x ARR)

### Defensibility Matrix

| Multiple | Valuation | Defensible | Rationale | Notes |
|----------|-----------|-----------|-----------|-------|
| **5x** | €900K | ❌ NO | Below market (B2B SaaS = 6-8x) | Reject unless special circumstance |
| **6x** | €1.08B | ✅ YES | Market baseline for €180K ARR | Conservative, safe bid |
| **7x** | €1.26B | ✅ YES | Slight premium on runway/team | Standard lead investor ask |
| **8x** | €1.44B | ✅ YES | High end of B2B SaaS range | Requires growth narrative |
| **10x** | €1.8B | ❌ NO | Requires >30% QoQ growth | Not defensible with €180K traction |

### Counter-Argument Playbook

**If investor proposes below 6x:**
> "Current B2B SaaS benchmarks (Benchling, Figma, Retool at Series A) are 6-8x ARR. At €180K ARR, below 6x undervalues the team's 12-month traction and de-risks Series B significantly."

**If investor proposes above 8x:**
> "To justify >8x, we'd need to demonstrate (1) >40% monthly growth, (2) Enterprise customer CAC payback <12 months, or (3) Irreplaceable IP moat. Current €180K traction supports 6-8x; we can revisit if growth accelerates to €50K/month."

**If investor requires equity restructuring:**
> "Proposed liquidation preference / board seat / anti-dilution [X] materially impacts exit math. Let's model the downside scenario: If we raise €1.08B at 20% dilution, founders retain 64%, which is standard."
