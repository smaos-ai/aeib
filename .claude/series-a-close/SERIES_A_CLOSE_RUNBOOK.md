**STEP 1: Confirm Meeting Details**
```

"Hi [Investor],

Looking forward to our meeting on [DAY/TIME] at [LOCATION]. 
Confirming details:
- Date/Time: [CONFIRM]
- Duration: 45 minutes
- Attendees (from our side): [List]
- Format: [In-person / Zoom / Hybrid]
- Agenda: [Product demo / Follow-up discussion / Term sheet discussion]

Please confirm if any of the above needs adjustment. 

Looking forward!
[NAME]"
```

**STEP 2: Prepare Deck & Materials**
- Deck tailored to investor thesis (if known)
  - Accel: Focus on product durability, TAM expansion
  - a16z: Focus on competitive advantage, network effects
  - Sapphire: Focus on enterprise sales, SaaS unit economics
- One-pager with key metrics (€180K ARR, growth rate, team)
- If follow-up: Slides addressing previous concerns

**STEP 3: Assign Roles**
- Primary speaker: CEO
- Product demo: [Lead Engineer / Product Lead]
- Q&A backup: CFO (for financial questions)
- Note-taker: [Administrative assistant] (captures sentiment, next steps)

### During Meeting (45 min)

**STRUCTURE:**
1. **Introduction** (3 min)
   - "Thanks for taking the time. Quick context: [1-sentence pitch]."
   - Investor background/relevance (why you're excited about them)

2. **Problem & Solution** (5 min)
   - Problem: [Specific pain point]
   - Solution: [How SovereignNexus solves it]
   - Market: [Size, growth, positioning]

3. **Product Demo** (15 min)
   - Live demo (aim for 2-3 key features, 5-minute max per feature)
   - Focus on what's differentiated
   - Handle objections smoothly ("Great question; here's how we handle that...")

4. **Traction & Metrics** (10 min)
   - Current traction: €180K ARR
   - Customer breakdown: [Types, growth rate]
   - Key metrics: [NRR, CAC payback, churn, monthly growth %]
   - Market validation: [Quotes, testimonials, win rates]

5. **Team & Execution** (5 min)
   - Founder backgrounds (why you're the right team)
   - Key hires made
   - Advisory board (if relevant)

6. **Ask & Timeline** (5 min)
   - We're raising €[X]M Series A
   - Timeline: Closing by [DATE]
   - Next step from your perspective?

7. **Q&A & Closing** (2 min)
   - "Any questions?" (pause for response)
   - "We'll send you [deck/one-pager] and happy to do a follow-up call next week if helpful."

## III. TERM SHEET NEGOTIATION PLAYBOOK

### Scenario 1: Favorable Term Sheet (High Valuation, Founder-Friendly)
**When:** Investor proposes 7-8x ARR, 1x non-participating, standard board seat

**ACTION:**
1. Schedule internal 30-minute call with board chair
2. Compare against other term sheets (if any pending)
3. Recommended response: "This is attractive. Let's schedule a call to confirm a few details on [ITEMS], and we can move to closing documents."

**Do not accept immediately** (even if favorable):
- Clarify board seat expectations
- Confirm pro-rata rights cap (typically 100-120%)
- Confirm closing timeline is achievable (14 days or less)

### Scenario 2: Medium Term Sheet (6x ARR, 1x participating)
**When:** Standard B2B SaaS terms, slightly founder-unfavorable

**COUNTER-OFFER STRATEGY:**

```
Email to investor (within 24 hours of receiving term sheet):

"Thanks for the term sheet. Great terms overall. A few items to discuss:

1. LIQUIDATION PREFERENCE
   Your proposal: 1x participating
   Our proposal: 1x non-participating
   Reasoning: Standard for Series A, aligns our incentives. Participation 
   erodes founder upside in M&A exits <€500M.
   
2. PRO-RATA RIGHTS CAP
   Your proposal: [Specify]
   Our proposal: 120% (allows you to maintain ownership in Series B)
   Reasoning: Standard for lead investors

3. CLOSING TIMELINE
   Your proposal: [X] days
   Our proposal: [14 business days]
   Reasoning: Achievable given legal work already underway

Can we schedule a call [SPECIFIC DAY/TIME] to discuss? I think 
we're quite aligned on valuation, and these are tweaks to standard 
language."
```

**If investor pushes back:**
- Liquidation preference: Offer "1x participating with 2x cap" as compromise
- Pro-rata rights: Offer "100% of pre-money investment" if they reject 120%
- Timeline: Offer "20 business days" if 14 is unrealistic

### Scenario 3: Unfavorable Term Sheet (Below 6x, Heavy Restrictions)
**When:** Investor proposes 5x ARR, 2x liquidation preference, 2 board seats

**DECISION TREE:**

```
Is this the only term sheet? 
├─ YES → Counter-offer aggressively; parallel track with other investors
└─ NO → Table discussion until other investors respond; keep in reserve

Counter-offer:
1. Valuation: Counter at 7x ARR (€1.26B)
   - If investor refuses, minimum acceptable: 6x ARR (€1.08B)
   - Walk threshold: <5.5x ARR (outside market range)

2. Liquidation: Counter with 1x non-participating
   - If investor insists: "1x non-participating with modified participation" 
     (capped at 2x investment, only if exit >€1B)
   - Walk threshold: Anything >2x (too punitive)

3. Board seats: Counter at 1 seat
   - If investor insists: Accept 1 + 1 information right
   - Walk threshold: >1 board seat for €270K check (not standard)

Email to investor:
"Thanks for the term sheet. Here are our counter-proposals:
[List items with reasoning]

We're excited about your commitment. Suggest we schedule 
a call to work through these collaboratively.

[Proposed times]"
```

**Negotiation Rules:**
- Always counter within 24 hours of receiving term sheet
- Do not accept first offer (signals willingness to move)
- Give 3-5 days for investor response before escalating
- If negotiations >1 week on same term sheet, escalate to investor partner/principal

## V. CRISIS RESPONSE PLAYBOOK

### Red Flag: Investor Due Diligence Hits on IP

**SCENARIO:** Investor's legal team says "we found IP concerns in our audit"

**IMMEDIATE RESPONSE (1 hour):**
1. CEO calls outside counsel: "What's the issue?"
2. Outside counsel identifies specific problem (e.g., "employee didn't sign IP assignment")
3. CEO schedules call with investor's counsel (same day)

**ESCALATION:**
```
If impact is MINOR (e.g., one missing signature):
  → Obtain signature immediately (48h)
  → Send signed doc to investor, request 24h clearance
  → Timeline: 3-4 days, no close delay

If impact is MODERATE (e.g., disputed IP from prior company):
  → Negotiate assignment / indemnification from founder
  → Escrow portion of Series A proceeds (e.g., 10%) for 90 days
  → Timeline: 7-10 days, close may slip 1 week

If impact is MAJOR (e.g., core technology is not company-owned):
  → Activate Board decision: raise separately or contest
  → May need to restart investor search
  → Timeline: 3-4 weeks
```

**Communication Template:**
```
Email to investor legal:

"We appreciate your due diligence review. Regarding the [IP ISSUE]:

Facts: [Description of situation]
Resolution: [Our plan to fix it]
Timeline: [We'll have signed/clear docs by DATE]

Proposed path forward: [Escrow if needed, or immediate resolution]

We're confident this won't impact closing timeline, but please 
let us know if you have questions."
```

---

### Red Flag: Investor Delays Final Signature (>5 days after term sheet)

**SCENARIO:** Investor says "term sheet in final legal review; expect docs [UNSPECIFIED DATE]"

**IMMEDIATE RESPONSE:**
1. Call investor partner (not junior associate): "When can we expect docs?"
2. If answer is >10 days out: "Our closing window is Jul 28. What's blocking you?"
3. Offer to accelerate: "Can we schedule a call with your legal team to unblock?"

**IF TIMELINE SLIPPING:**
- Pivot to backup investor (if term sheet pending)
- Parallel track: Set hard deadline: "If we don't have final docs by [DATE], we move with other investor"
- Email to investor: "We're excited about your offer. To keep momentum, we need executed docs by [DATE]. Can you commit?"

**ESCALATION:**
```
DAY 1: Partner-to-partner call
DAY 3: If no progress, CEO calls investor's General Partner
DAY 5: If no movement, activate backup lead investor
DAY 7: Make decision: proceed with alternative or extend timeline to August
```

---

### Red Flag: Investor Wants to Reduce Valuation (Downround signal)

**SCENARIO:** Investor calls: "New data suggests we should re-price at 5x. Can we discuss?"

**DECISION TREE:**

```
Is this the ONLY investor close to signing?
├─ YES → 
│   ├─ Call board chair immediately
│   ├─ If you have term sheet from another investor: 
│   │   "We have another offer at 6x. We'll proceed with them."
│   ├─ If this is only offer:
│   │   ├─ Counter at 6x minimum
│   │   ├─ If investor won't budge: Walk or defer close
│   │   └─ Alert board: potential downround vs. no funding
│   
└─ NO →
    └─ Response: "We have other strong offers at 6-7x. 
        We're going to proceed with those. If you want to participate 
        at market terms, let us know in 48h."
```

**DO NOT ACCEPT valuation <6x without board approval** (implies negative signal about company performance).
