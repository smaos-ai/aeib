## INVESTOR LISTENING FRAMEWORK

**What investors listen for in reference calls:**

1. **Authenticity:** Does CTO sound like they genuinely use SMAOS, or are they reading a script?
   - Good signal: Specific examples, problems in their own words
   - Bad signal: Vague talking points, scripted language

2. **ROI:** Can CTO articulate quantifiable impact (speed, cost, compliance)?
   - Good signal: "Went from €70k/month to €2k/month"
   - Bad signal: "It's great" (vague)

3. **Commitment:** Is customer sticking around? What's expansion likelihood?
   - Good signal: "Already scaled from 1 pilot to 20 end-customers"
   - Bad signal: "Let's see how it goes" (uncommitted)

4. **Competitive validation:** Why SMAOS over alternatives?
   - Good signal: "Only platform doing pre-gate safety enforcement"
   - Bad signal: "We didn't really look at competitors"

5. **Reference-ability:** Will CTO stay available for due diligence calls?
   - Good signal: "Happy to talk to your technical team"
   - Bad signal: "This is my only call available"
