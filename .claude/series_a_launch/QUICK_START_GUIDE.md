## COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

❌ **Mistake 1:** Sending generic intro to all 50 investors (same body text)  
✅ **Fix:** Personalize each email (use investor fund name, mention specific interest like "security" or "SaaS")

❌ **Mistake 2:** No Calendly link (makes scheduling friction)  
✅ **Fix:** Always include Calendly link, make it first thing investor sees

❌ **Mistake 3:** Sharing financial model before LOI (gives away negotiating power)  
✅ **Fix:** Share after intro call, full numbers only after NDA

❌ **Mistake 4:** Not tracking outreach (lose follow-up momentum)  
✅ **Fix:** Log every send in Excel, set reminder for Day 3 + Day 7 follow-up

❌ **Mistake 5:** Overselling (sounds desperate, reduces credibility)  
✅ **Fix:** Lead with traction (3 paying pilots), let unit economics speak
