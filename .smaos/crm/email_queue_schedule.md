## INBOX OPTIMIZATION STRATEGY

### Why Segment Timing Matters

**Morning Sends (0800 UTC = 09:00 CET / 03:00 EDT):**
- Captures EU morning inbox (primary market)
- Hits US morning queue (secondary market)
- Higher open rates (8-11am is peak email engagement)
- Action-oriented investors open morning emails first

**Evening Sends (1600 UTC = 17:00 CET / 11:00 EDT):**
- Catches EU end-of-day readers (executive inbox reviews)
- Hits US mid-morning (decision-makers still active)
- Avoids Friday email avalanche (Thursday evening send)
- Evening reads often lead to Friday morning meetings

**Weekend Sends (Saturday 0800 UTC, Saturday 1600 UTC, Sunday 1400 UTC):**
- Lower competition inbox (fewer emails Saturday/Sunday)
- Defense + government investors read weekends (prep Monday)
- Startup teams read email on weekends (catch founder directly)
- Non-responders see follow-up = re-engagement signal
