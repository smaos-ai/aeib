## RESPONSE HANDLING (Post-Reply)

**If investor responds positively:**
1. **Same-day reply** (within 1 hour if possible)
2. **Confirm Calendly time** (or offer 3 time slots)
3. **Send pre-call prep materials** (one-pager + pitch deck link)
4. **CRM update:** Mark as "Engaged - Meeting Booked" + confidence score

**If investor responds with objection:**
1. **Address objection directly** (not defensive)
2. **Share relevant proof point** (Renko MRR, patent validation, deployment traction)
3. **Offer alternative call time or format** (30-min vs. 20-min, phone vs. video)
4. **CRM update:** Mark as "Engaged - Objection Noted" + note for call

**If investor responds with "not now":**
1. **Ask for defer timeline** ("Circle back in month 12 for Series B?")
2. **Offer to send post-Series A update** (July 1, with closed round details)
3. **CRM update:** Mark as "Deferred - Series B" + date to re-engage
