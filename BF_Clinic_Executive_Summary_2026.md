## THE OPPORTUNITY

Launch a **world-class aesthetic surgery clinic in Brno, Czech Republic** that trains surgeons (fellowship program) and funds reconstruction for Ukrainian war victims. 

**Market position:** Czech Republic offers 40-60% cost advantage vs Western Europe while maintaining EU-standard medical quality. Brno has 17 competitors but zero fellowship training programs.

---

## THE BUSINESS (3-YEAR PLAN)

### Phase 1: Clinic Launch (Months 1-6)
- Lease surgical facility in Brno (€8-12K/month)
- Hire: 1-2 surgeons, 1 anesthetist, 3-4 nursing staff
- Setup: OR equipment, imaging, patient portal
- **Revenue target:** €366K Year 1 (300+ surgeries), break-even Month 4-5

### Phase 1B: Fellowship Pilot (Month 6+)
- Recruit 2-3 international fellows (training surgeons)
- Structure: 6-12 month paid training program
- Revenue model: Charge fellows €15-30K/year, offset with clinic revenue sharing
- **Partnership:** Masaryk University (thesis/research credit)

### Phase 2: Scale + Fundraising (Year 2)
- Add 2nd surgeon + expand capacity to 600+ surgeries/year
- Launch YouTube channel (thought leadership, inbound patients)
- Seek €500K-1M seed round from EU/Ukrainian impact investors
- **Revenue target:** €1M+ (clinic + fellowship + research partnerships)

### Phase 3: Charitable Fund (Year 3+)
- Allocate 10% of clinic profits to fund reconstruction surgery for Ukrainian victims
- Target: €50-100K/year charitable support
- Marketing: "Every procedure funds a Ukrainian reconstruction" → strong brand differentiation
