#### **3. Razom for Ukraine — "Face the Future" Missions**

**Organization:** US-based nonprofit focused on Ukraine medical support  
**Program:** "Face the Future Ukraine" (international surgical missions)  
**Model:** Partner clinics host international surgeons for short missions  
**Recent activity (May 2026):** 68 surgeries in Ivano-Frankivsk, 24 patients treated  

**How partnership works:**
1. Razom identifies Ukrainian patients needing facial reconstruction
2. Razom recruits international surgeons (volunteer or funded)
3. Razom funds patient travel + accommodation
4. Partner clinic (e.g., BF Clinic) provides surgical facility
5. Local doctors apprentice/train during mission
6. Razom covers logistics; partner absorbs partial cost

**Financial model:**
- Example: Surgery costs CZK 50K (€2K)
- Razom pays: €1,200-1,500
- Clinic donates: €500-800 (as charitable contribution)
- Revenue to clinic: €1,200-1,500 per surgery
- Training value: Ukrainian surgeons get apprenticeship

**How to partner:**
- Contact: partnerships@razomforukraine.org
- Pitch: "Become preferred surgical partner for international missions"
- Expected response time: 2-4 weeks
- Estimated volume: 3-5 surgeries/month (if partnership approved)
