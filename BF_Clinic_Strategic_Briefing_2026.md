## 2.4 Medical Device & Surgical Instrument Compliance (EU MDR)

**Current Status:** ✅ **COMPLIANT** — Using existing instruments

### Context
If you use **existing, CE-marked surgical instruments** (standard scalpels, liposuction cannulas, implants), you have **zero regulatory burden**. These are Class I or Class II devices already registered in EUDAMED.

### If Developing Novel Instruments (Phase 2+)
**Timeline:** 6-12 months before market  
**Complexity:** Moderate

If you design new surgical instruments (e.g., custom micro-surgical tools, patented liposuction cannulas), you must:

1. **Classify device** (Class I, IIa, IIb, or III)
   - Most surgical instruments = Class II (moderate risk)
   - Requires Notified Body involvement

2. **File with EUDAMED** (EU database)
   - Deadline for new devices: May 28, 2026 ✅ (Already passed; next deadline Nov 28, 2026)
   - Cost: €5K-15K (Notified Body fees)
   - Timeline: 3-6 months

3. **CE Mark attestation**
   - Manufacturer declares conformity
   - Notified Body performs technical review (if Class II+)
   - CE mark applied to device

### Action Items (Month 6+, if pursuing patent innovations)
- [ ] Identify novel instruments/techniques to patent
- [ ] Consult EU MDR specialist (Prague/Brno-based medical device legal firm)
- [ ] File provisional patents (low-cost, fast, protects IP)
- [ ] Plan Notified Body engagement for CE marking (parallel track)

## 4.6 Pricing Strategy: Key Decisions

### Decision: Volume vs. Margin?
**Answer:** Volume (short-term), then margin optimization (long-term)

**Rationale:**
- Build patient base quickly → word-of-mouth accelerates → referral rate increases
- Achieve scale → cost per surgery decreases → margin improves naturally
- Year 1: Accept 10-15% net margin (reinvest in team/marketing)
- Year 2: Target 20%+ margin (as volume increases)

### Decision: Premium international patients vs. local Czech market?
**Answer:** Local Czech + Ukrainian diaspora (prioritize accessible market)

**Rationale:**
- Aligns with mission ("quality for low price")
- Builds sustainable volume (local market = repeat patients, referrals)
- International medical tourism is commoditized (can pursue later, Year 2+)
- Ukrainian connection supports Phase 3 charitable narrative

### Decision: Insurance billing?
**Answer:** No (initially). Aesthetic surgery is not covered; overhead not worth it.

**Rationale:**
- Czech health insurance explicitly excludes cosmetic procedures
- Insurance billing requires admin overhead (claims processing, denials, appeals)
- Private pay + clear pricing is simpler and aligns with "honest pricing" mission
- Revisit in Year 2 if volume requires admin scaling
