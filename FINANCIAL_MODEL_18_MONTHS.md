## Unit Economics

### Enterprise Segment

| Metric | Value | Reasoning |
|--------|-------|-----------|
| **ACV (Annual Contract Value)** | €700k | Blended across 3-year + 1-year pilots |
| **CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)** | €50k | PoC effort + compliance work (3-4 months) |
| **CAC Payback Period** | 8.6 months | 50k / (700k/12) |
| **LTV (Lifetime Value)** | €2.1M | 3-year contract, 5% annual churn, 85% gross margin |
| **LTV/CAC Ratio** | 42:1 | Highly profitable enterprise model |
| **Sales Cycle** | 6-9 months | Compliance/regulatory driven (long sales) |
| **Retention (Year 2+)** | 95% | High switching costs (re-certification = €50-100k) |

**Enterprise TAM Penetration:**
- Year 1: 6 customers = 0.012% of €50B Defense/Healthcare/Finance TAM
- Year 1.5: 15 customers = 0.03% of €50B TAM
- Year 3: 100+ customers = 0.2% of €50B TAM
- **Realistic ceiling (Year 5):** 500-1,000 customers = 1-2% penetration

### Creator Platform Segment

| Metric | Value | Reasoning |
|--------|-------|-----------|
| **ACV (Annual Contract Value)** | €408 | Blended across cohorts (€300-500 range) |
| **CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)** | €10k | Paid ads, content marketing, affiliate (1-month to recoup) |
| **CAC Payback Period** | 11.8 months | 10k / (408/12) |
| **LTV (Lifetime Value)** | €1.5k | 2-year retention, 2% monthly churn, 85% gross margin |
| **LTV/CAC Ratio** | 0.15:1 | Requires scale + retention optimization |
| **Churn Rate (Monthly)** | 2% | Platform economy (creators leave for competitors) |
| **Viral Coefficient** | 1.2 | Word-of-mouth + creator communities |
| **Unit Economics Breakeven** | 5k creators | Break-even on platform infrastructure + support |

**Creator Platform Path to Profitability:**
- Month 1-6: Subsidy mode (CAC > LTV)
- Month 7-12: Near breakeven (viral growth + retention improvements)
- Month 13-24: Profitable (scale CAC to €5k, improve LTV via retention)
- **Year 3+:** Potential €100M+ market (10-15% of €1T creator economy)
