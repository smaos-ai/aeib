- **Sales cycle:** 2–4 months (pilot → contract → deployment)

## Financial Model Assumptions & Caveats

### Key Assumptions
1. **Sales cycle:** 2–4 months (pilot → contract → deployment)
2. **Churn rate:** 5% annually (enterprise), 40% annually (creators)
3. **NRR:** 110% (10% expansion revenue from upsell/cross-sell)
4. **Infrastructure cost:** 15% of revenue (auto-scales with customer base)
5. **Model licensing:** €2.50/inference, 100M inferences/year by Month 18
6. **CAC:** €20K (includes sales + marketing + onboarding)
7. **Payroll inflation:** 2%/year
8. **No dilution events:** Assumes clean capital structure

### Sensitivity Drivers
- **Customer acquisition rate:** ±50% swing = €43M–€87M ARR impact
- **ASP (Average Selling Price):** ±20% change = ±€16M ARR impact
- **Infrastructure cost:** If cloud costs rise 20% → EBITDA margin drops from 71% to 67%
- **Churn acceleration:** If churn rises to 10% (from 5%), NRR drops to 100%, ARR impacts Year 2+
- **Regulatory delays:** If compliance takes 6+ months, CAC increases 25%, extends cash flow positive to Month 18

### Not Included (Out of Scope)
- Tax effects (assume post-tax EBITDA ~70% of gross EBITDA)
- CapEx amortization (modeled as OpEx)
- Foreign exchange risk (EUR-based, USD exposure unhedged)
- Strategic M&A, partnerships, or licensing revenue
- Government contracts or procurement delays
