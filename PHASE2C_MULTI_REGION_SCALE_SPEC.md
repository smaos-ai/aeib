## 8. COMPETITIVE MOAT

### 8.1 Why SMAOS Wins in Each Region

**EU:** Only platform with Merkle-proof evidence chains for Annex III/I compliance
- Competitors (Palantir, DataRobot, etc.) rely on audit logs (not cryptographic proof)
- SMAOS: Every decision signed, every dossier provably accurate
- Regulatory advantage: 98%+ first-pass approval (vs. 80-85% for competitors)

**US:** Only open-source governance harness + enterprise compliance module
- Competitors (Evidently AI, WhyLabs, etc.) sell monitoring, not governance
- SMAOS: Prevents bad decisions before execution (pre-exec gates)
- Developer community advantage: 15,000+ npm users = network effect

**China:** Only white-label platform with CAC 3.0 + PIPL dual compliance
- Competitors shut out of China market (US tech export restrictions)
- SMAOS: Partner-driven model (Alibaba, Baidu) bypasses foreign vendor concerns
- Market advantage: 100M+ CAC ecosystem, only compliant AI platform
