## 2. GOVERNANCE & CONTROL

### 2.1 Board of Directors
- **Founder:** 1 seat
- **Investor (Lead):** 1 seat
- **Investor (Pro-Rata, if €5M+):** Observation rights
- **Mutual Selection:** 1 independent director (agreed by lead investor + founder)

### 2.2 Protective Provisions
Investor has veto rights on:
- Liquidation, merger, or sale of company
- Amendment of cap table or articles of association
- Issuance of new share classes senior to Series A
- Dividend declaration or redemption
- Change of business or dissolution
- Related-party transactions >€50k
- Board composition changes

### 2.3 Information Rights
- **Quarterly:** Unaudited financial statements + board presentation (within 30 days of quarter close)
- **Annual:** Audited financial statements (within 90 days of fiscal year close)
- **On-Demand:** Reasonable inspection rights + founder certification of compliance with covenants

### 2.4 Anti-Dilution Protection
**Broad-based weighted average:**
- Adjustment applies to Series A conversion price if new equity issued below Series A price
- Formula: New conversion price = Old price × (Fully diluted pre-money + new investment) / (Fully diluted pre-money + new investment at new price)
- Exceptions: Employee stock options (10% pool), ESOP conversions, stock splits/recapitalizations

### 2.5 Liquidation Preference
**1x Non-Participating Preferred:** Investor receives €[Series A amount] + accrued dividends (8% annual) before common shareholders in liquidation event.

**Waterfall:**
1. Debt holders (if any)
2. Series A Preferred (1x liquidation preference)
3. Remaining proceeds → Common shareholders (founder, employees, advisors)

**Participation:** If investor elects, Series A can convert to common and participate in remaining proceeds (non-participating is typical for later rounds).
