## 3. Corrected Architectural Boundaries

1. **Untrusted Reasoning Telemetry:**
   - Model reasoning traces (`<think>` blocks, Chain-of-Thought) are treated as untrusted telemetry.
   - Authorization gates evaluate solely the signed Act envelope, cryptographic capability token, policy epoch, taint tier, and content-addressed CAID.
2. **Explicit Workflow Preconditions:**
   - Replaced heuristic post-hoc checks with versioned workflow invariants (`workflow_preconditions`). Consequential actions (e.g. `book_seat`) fail closed if required prerequisites (e.g. `check_seat_availability`) lack a confirmed ledger receipt.
3. **Scoped Forgetting Invariant:**
   - Implemented `execute_scoped_forget`: agent scratchpads, candidate drafts, and transient search history are discarded to mitigate conservative anchoring, while 100% of the authoritative ledger, audit receipts, and unresolved `EFFECT_INDETERMINATE` states are preserved.
4. **Lean Cryptography & Realistic PQC Scope:**
   - Real Ed25519 (RFC 8032) and SHA-256 are used for current verification.
   - ML-DSA-65 (FIPS 204) and Poseidon zero-knowledge proof circuits are explicitly classified as follow-on workstreams, pending binding integration with verified native cryptographic libraries.
