**Effective date:** Jun 9, 2026 (stock purchase date, after incorporation confirmed)  
**Parties:** All founders + SovereignNexus, Inc. (signed by CEO)  
**Governing law:** State of Delaware

## 2. Vesting Schedule

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Grant date | Jun 9, 2026 (stock purchase date) |
| Total vesting period | 4 years |
| Cliff | 1 year (25% vests on Jun 9, 2027) |
| Post-cliff vesting | 1/48th of total shares per month |
| Acceleration trigger | Double-trigger only — Change of Control + Involuntary Termination |
| Single-trigger acceleration | Not applicable |

**Vesting schedule example (4,000,000 shares):**

```
Jun 9, 2027 (cliff):  1,000,000 shares vest (25%)
Jul 9, 2027:            83,333 shares vest (1/48 per month)
Aug 9, 2027:            83,333 shares vest
...
Jun 9, 2030:          4,000,000 shares fully vested
```

**Double-trigger definition:**  
Trigger 1: Change of Control (acquisition, merger, asset sale resulting in >50% ownership change)  
Trigger 2: Involuntary termination without cause, or resignation for good reason, within 12 months of Change of Control  
Both triggers must occur for acceleration to apply.

## 3. Restrictive Covenants

### Non-Solicitation

Duration: 18 months post-departure  
Scope:
- **Employees:** Founders shall not solicit, recruit, or hire any employee or contractor of SovereignNexus, Inc.
- **Customers:** Founders shall not solicit or divert any customer or business partner of SovereignNexus, Inc. with whom founder had material contact during the prior 12 months of service

### Non-Compete

Delaware courts generally do not enforce broad non-compete agreements. This agreement does **not** include a general non-compete clause.

### Confidentiality

Duration: **Perpetual**  
Scope: All non-public information relating to SovereignNexus, Inc. business, technology, customers, finances, and strategy, including but not limited to:
- AXIOM and SIGP patent details, trade secrets, and implementation details
- SISS architecture and source code
- Customer lists, contracts, and pricing
- Series A materials, investor communications, and financial projections

Exceptions: Information that becomes publicly known through no fault of the founder, or information that must be disclosed by law (with prior written notice to the company where legally permitted).

## 4. Tax Elections

- Each founder receiving restricted stock **must** timely file an 83(b) election with the IRS
- Filing deadline: **Jul 9, 2026** (30 days from Jun 9, 2026 stock issuance)
- Method: IRS Form 15620 — https://www.irs.gov/forms/form-15620
- Founder acknowledges that failure to timely file results in ordinary income tax liability at each vesting event, potentially resulting in material tax exposure at Series A valuation
- Each founder represents that they have received independent tax advice or knowingly waived such advice

## 5. Representations and Warranties

Each founder represents that:
- They have the right and authority to enter into this agreement
- The IP being assigned does not infringe any third-party rights
- No other agreements conflict with this agreement (e.g., prior employer IP assignment agreements)
- They are not party to any non-compete or IP assignment that restricts their contribution to SovereignNexus, Inc.

---

## 6. Termination and Repurchase Rights

- Company retains right to repurchase unvested shares at cost ($0.0001/share) upon founder departure
- Vested shares are not subject to company repurchase except pursuant to a Right of First Refusal (ROFR)
- ROFR: Before any transfer of shares, founder must offer shares to the company at the proposed transfer price
