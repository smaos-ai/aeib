## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

SovereignNexus addresses the €100B+ APAC critical infrastructure + defense + government AI market driven by sovereign compute, national security concerns, and AUKUS alliance infrastructure strategy. APAC governments (Australia, Japan, South Korea, India, Singapore) face geopolitical AI supply chain risk: US LLM dominance (OpenAI, Google) creates dependency on foreign vendors. SovereignNexus provides deterministic, auditable, local-first AI governance for defense and critical infrastructure operators who cannot rely on US-based cloud AI.

**Market Thesis:** APAC governments are transitioning from caution to commitment on sovereign AI infrastructure (€160B+ hyperscaler investment in APAC). However, government agencies deploying AI face unprecedented security risk: every decision goes through US-based LLM APIs (model data extraction, compliance proofs). SovereignNexus eliminates that risk via deterministic, locally-executable governance layer with zero exfiltration architecture.

**Go-To-Market:** Government relations first (CIO/CTO at defense agencies, critical infrastructure operators), infrastructure partnerships (with regional integrators + defense contractors), multi-year fixed contracts. 6–12 month sales cycles.

## 5. PRICING MODEL & JUSTIFICATION

### 5.1 Selected Model: Fixed Enterprise Licensing + Audit Support

**Why Fixed (Not Per-Decision)?**
- Government procurement requires predictable costs (budget approval takes 6–12 months)
- Per-decision pricing creates unpredictable government budgeting (risks contract cancellation)
- Defense agencies prefer fixed SLAs (cost + security guarantees)

**Why Audit Support Included?**
- Government must audit AI decisions (parliamentary accountability)
- Audit support (proof generation, verification) is cost of doing business with government
- Bundling audit into licensing aligns incentives

**Why Multi-Year?**
- Government procurement favors 3–5 year contracts (procurement efficiency)
- Multi-year contracts create predictable revenue for SovereignNexus
- Annual reviews enable scope expansion (pilots → multi-agency, multi-region)

- Impact: Delays Nov conversion, but builds stronger credibility for 2027 government sales.

_If Booz Allen, Leidos, Thales don't engage (Oct gate at risk)_
