## 5. PRICING MODEL & JUSTIFICATION

### 5.1 Selected Model: Hybrid (Per-Entity + Execution-Based)

**Why Not Pure Per-User?**
- EU enterprises have complex org structures (subsidiaries, business units, autonomous teams)
- Per-user creates perverse incentive: enterprises hide deployments to avoid cost
- Governance is _better_ when deployed across full organization (transparency)

**Why Not Pure Subscription?**
- Customers don't understand governance value until they see cost savings
- Subscription makes ROI hard to measure
- Execution-based creates natural cost sharing (customer benefits from decisions → pays for them)

**Why Hybrid?**
- Per-entity base: Covers overhead, support, compliance
- Per-decision execution: Aligns incentives, scales with value
- Combination enables both predictability (base) + usage growth (execution)
