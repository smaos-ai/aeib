## Strategic Framework

The APAC GTM follows the same compliance-wedge playbook that proved effective in the EU (Stream 6, GDPR/NIS2 moat): enter via a regulatory trigger that creates an immediate, non-deferrable purchase decision, establish 1–2 anchor enterprise pilots, then expand via partner referrals. The difference from EU: APAC regulatory frameworks are less mature and more advisory, so we lead with enterprise ROI (audit cost reduction, compliance-as-a-service) alongside compliance positioning.

**GTM motion per market:**
1. Summit presence (media + peer visibility) — creates pipeline awareness
2. Partner anchor (EDB / HKSTP / CSIRO) — provides institutional credibility + warm intros
3. 5-day on-site mission (CTO + sales lead) — closes first pilot agreements
4. Hub hire (local account executive) — sustains pipeline post-mission

**Sales cycle assumption:** Enterprise pilot agreement in 45–60 days from first meeting. Pilots are 3-month contracts at €15–25K (pilot fee covers deployment + onboarding). Successful pilots convert to 12-month contracts at €100–300K ARR.

## Partner Strategy Detail

### Singapore — EDB Global Innovation Alliance

**Program:** EDB co-funds market entry activities for select foreign technology companies. Benefits include:
- Co-marketing with EDB brand (credibility multiplier with Singaporean enterprises)
- Introductions to MAS-regulated entities via EDB financial services network
- Potential co-funding of first local hire (up to S$30K)
- Access to EDB's "AI Singapore" initiative — opens government pilot procurement channels

**Application requirements:** Company must be incorporated or willing to incorporate a Singapore subsidiary. Revenue threshold: none for innovation program. Timeline: 6–8 weeks approval.

**Action:** Submit EDB GIA application by Jul 15, 2026. Target: approval by Sep 1 (pre-summit).

### Hong Kong — HKSTP Innovation Ecosystem

**Program:** HKSTP InnoHK — provides office space, tenant introductions, and co-marketing for technology companies.

**Benefits:**
- Co-working space in Pak Shek Kok Science Park (reduces HK office cost)
- Introductions to HKSTP tenant companies (250+ companies — immediate sales pipeline)
- HKSTP "Portfolio Company" designation (government credibility badge)
- Connections to Innovation and Technology Commission (ITC)

**Application:** HKSTP Incu-Tech program application. Timeline: 4–6 weeks. No revenue minimum.

**Action:** Submit HKSTP application by Aug 1, 2026.

### Australia — CSIRO OPAL Partnership

**Program:** CSIRO Data61 OPAL commercial partnership — CSIRO is actively seeking commercial partners to implement OPAL in production government deployments.

**Benefits:**
- Co-presentation rights at government events (opens APS procurement channels)
- CSIRO technical support for OPAL integration
- Warm introductions to 5+ federal government agencies
- Potential CSIRO co-investment in APAC R&D activities

**Engagement plan:**
1. Initial contact: Data61 commercialization team (data61.csiro.au) — Jul 2026
2. Technical alignment meeting: Aug 2026 (in-person Canberra or Sydney)
