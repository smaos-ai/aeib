## INVESTOR TYPE PROFILES

### Profile A: Tier-1 VC (Sequoia, Accel, Summit, Lightspeed)
**Typical Allocations:** €50M-500M+ per round  
**Decision Timeline:** 4-8 weeks  
**Tech Knowledge:** High (often C-suite founders on LPs)  
**Interests:** Market size, founder quality, competitive defensibility, unit economics

**MUST-HAVE PACKET:**
- [ ] Pitch Deck (PDF, 15 slides, 5 MB max)
- [ ] Market Research summary (top 3 pages of `/data_room/Market_Research_450M_TAM.md`)
- [ ] Financial Model 5-year (spreadsheet)
- [ ] Due Diligence Summary (1 PDF)
- [ ] Executive bio (1 page per founder)
- [ ] Proof of concept artifacts (screenshots: pitch deck render, pilot screenshot, Q&A)

**OPTIONAL (if requested):**
- [ ] Full architecture document (GitHub PDF export)
- [ ] Technical brief (1 page, for CTO due diligence)
- [ ] Competitive analysis (detailed tier-by-tier)
- [ ] Customer logos + testimonials (if any pilot customers signed NDA)
- [ ] IP portfolio summary (patents, trademarks)

**DISTRIBUTION METHOD:**
- Email: Initial intro email with Pitch Deck (PDF) + one-pager
- Data room link: Send secure Dropbox/Box link with NDA
- In-person: Bring physical leave-behind (Pitch deck print + financial model USB)

**FOLLOW-UP TIMELINE:**
- T+1: Check delivery, confirm receipt via Slack
- T+5: "Did you have questions on the market data?"
- T+10: "Ready to schedule 30-min intro call?"
- T+15: "Can I set up your team's technical diligence?"

### Profile B: Strategic Investor (Banks, Fintech, Insurance)
**Typical Allocations:** €5M-50M  
**Decision Timeline:** 8-12 weeks (internal approval slower)  
**Tech Knowledge:** Medium (product/compliance focus > engineering)  
**Interests:** Regulatory fit, pilot success, integration roadmap, reference customers

**MUST-HAVE PACKET:**
- [ ] Pitch Deck (slides 1-10 only: Problem, Solution, Market, Traction)
- [ ] Governance Brief (1-pager on pre-execution veto gates)
- [ ] Market Brief (TAM for their vertical: banking/insurance/asset mgmt)
- [ ] Financial Model (Years 1-3 only, unit economics)
- [ ] Due Diligence Summary (compliance angle)
- [ ] Pilot case study (1-2 page summary of hotel credit pilot)

**OPTIONAL (if engaged):**
- [ ] Full pitch deck (all 17 slides)
- [ ] Architecture summary (non-technical version: "how it stops bad agent actions")
- [ ] Regulatory roadmap (EU AI Act timeline + their vertical's deadlines)
- [ ] Customer reference intro (UniCredit or Czech National Bank endorsement letter)
- [ ] Integration roadmap (APIs, SWIFT compatibility, etc.)

**DISTRIBUTION METHOD:**
- Email: Warm intro via their board member or advisor
- Meeting: In-person pitch (30 min) + leave-behind folder
- Data room: Secure portal access (after NDA + legal review)

**FOLLOW-UP TIMELINE:**
- T+3: Send additional materials if requested during pitch
- T+7: "Can we arrange technical deep-dive with your risk/compliance team?"
- T+14: "Interest in a 1-week pilot on your treasury desk?"
- T+21: "Ready to discuss partnership + minority stake opportunity?"

**Interests:** Regulatory compliance, sovereign AI, human control, geographical impact

**DISTRIBUTION METHOD:**
- Email: Direct warm intro from LP or partner

### Profile D: Angel / Early-Stage Investor
**Typical Allocations:** €100K-€1M  
**Decision Timeline:** 2-4 weeks  
**Tech Knowledge:** High variance (check LinkedIn)  
**Interests:** Founder story, market opportunity, "getting in early"

**MUST-HAVE PACKET:**
- [ ] 2-page one-pager (Problem + Solution + Founders)
- [ ] Pitch Deck (full 17 slides, PDF)
- [ ] Financial Model (simple 3-year projection)
- [ ] Executive bios (emphasis on track record)
- [ ] Market opportunity (TAM section)

**OPTIONAL:**
- [ ] Technical brief (1 page)
- [ ] Competitive landscape (who else is building this?)

**DISTRIBUTION METHOD:**
- Email: Initial 1-pager + Pitch Deck PDF (attach both)
- Meeting: Short call (15-20 min)
- Relationship: Coffee/drinks + conversation (no formal materials needed)

**FOLLOW-UP TIMELINE:**
- T+1: "Did you review the deck? Happy to jump on a call anytime"
- T+5: "Any questions I can answer?"
- T+10: "We're closing a friends-and-family round by [date] if you're interested"

- T+3: Introduce product lead from their side

## PREPARATION CHECKLIST (Before Sending)

### Quality Assurance
- [ ] All PDFs < 5 MB (compress images if needed)
- [ ] All file names standardized: `SMAOS_[TITLE]_v1.0.pdf`
- [ ] All links in docs tested (Notion, GitHub, etc. accessible?)
- [ ] Deck renders cleanly on recipient's screen (test PDF render)
- [ ] Financial model macros/formulas work in their Excel/Sheets

### Compliance & Legal
- [ ] NDA signed before sending sensitive materials (Architecture, Budget)
- [ ] Confidentiality footer added: "© SovereignNexus GmbH, Confidential - Do Not Distribute"
- [ ] No personal emails or internal Slack links in documents
- [ ] All trade secrets marked as such (IP portfolio, customer lists)

### Personalization
- [ ] Recipient name in cover email (not "Dear Investor")
- [ ] Custom pitch opening: "Based on your focus on [domain], we thought you'd appreciate..."
- [ ] 1-2 sentence bio of mutual connection (if applicable)
- [ ] Clear call-to-action: "I'd love to grab 20 min next week to walk through the deck"

### Tracking
- [ ] Document delivery logged in CRM (Salesforce, HubSpot)
- [ ] Send via email with read receipt enabled
- [ ] Send via secure link (Docsend, Box, Dropbox) with tracking enabled
- [ ] Log investor contact info (name, title, email, phone, focus area)
- [ ] Set calendar reminder for follow-up email (T+5 days)

## POST-MEETING FOLLOW-UP

### After Pitch Meeting
1. Send thank-you email within 24h
2. Attach Q&A briefing (answers to questions they asked)
3. Include 2-3 additional materials they requested
4. Propose next step: "Technical deep-dive scheduled for [date]?"

### After Diligence Meeting
1. Send minutes + action items (mutual sign-off)
2. Provide additional data requested (comps analysis, customer refs, etc.)
3. Propose term sheet discussion: "Shall we get legal teams talking?"

### After Term Sheet Negotiations
1. Send executed term sheet + side letters
2. Introduce to investor's board counsel
3. Begin onboarding: "Who else should be on the cap table review call?"

## QUESTIONS TO PREPARE FOR

### From Tier-1 VCs
- "Who are your top 3 competitors, and how do you beat them?"
- "What's your customer acquisition cost? How do you scale from 3 pilots to 1,000 customers?"
- "If Microsoft or AWS builds this in 6 months, how do you survive?"
- "What's your unit economics at 100 customers? 1,000?"
- "How do you defend against open-source alternatives?"

**See** `/qa/INVESTOR_QA_BRIEFING.md` **for full answers**

### From Strategic Investors
- "How hard is it to integrate with our SWIFT interface?"
- "Do you handle our compliance requirements (MiFID, GDPR, DMA)?"
- "Can we white-label this for our customers?"
- "What happens if your infrastructure goes down? Do agents block or fall through?"

### From Impact Funds
- "How many human decisions does this system enable per year across your customers?"
- "Can you prove that regulatory agencies actually trust your signatures?"
- "What's your carbon footprint (hardware + cloud)?"
- "How does this work in low-income markets where internet is unreliable?"
